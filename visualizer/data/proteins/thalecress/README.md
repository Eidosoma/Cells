- **What a plant cell adds.** The plastid has two levels. Its envelope, two membranes standing as one shelf, has the stroma inside it and the cytoplasm beyond: a protein in either membrane is in the envelope, and a protein free in the stroma stands on that shelf, inside. The thylakoid membrane lies in the stroma, with its lumen inside and the stroma beyond: a protein held at it and facing the stroma stands on the thylakoid's shelf, outside. The vacuole has a shelf of its own. A protein of the wall or of the space outside the cell membrane stands on the cell membrane's shelf, outside, as a secreted protein does, and so does one of the plasmodesmata, which join a plant's cells.
