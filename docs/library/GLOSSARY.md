# Glossary

Each term in plain words, then in the words that biology, chemistry, physics and computing use
for the same thing. A word of Cells' own says where it is defined. The pages link a term at its
first use, and each entry's "Used by" line, generated by `scripts/library_pages.py --write`, lists
the pages that link it.

### Abundance

How much of a channel or pump population sits on a patch, as a factor that scales the
population's rate. It is a value of the definition, not of the law.

- **In other fields:** expression level or density (biology); surface concentration of the
  protein (chemistry); a weight on a rate (physics); a per-population input array (computing).
- **Unit:** none (a factor).
- **Cells' word;** see [the inner-model design](../INNER_MODEL_V4_DESIGN.md).

### ATP drive

The free energy that one ATP molecule releases when it is split into ADP and phosphate, at the
concentrations where it is split. Cells writes it $\Delta_r G'_\text{ATP}$. It is negative when ATP
can drive a cycle, and the more negative it is, the harder it drives.

- **In other fields:** the cell's energy charge (biology); the reaction free energy of ATP
  hydrolysis (chemistry); a chemical potential difference that can do work (physics); one input of
  a transporter's rate (computing).
- **Unit:** J/mol.

<!-- generated: used-by atp-drive -->

- **Used by:** no page yet.

<!-- /generated -->

### Availability

A number from 0 to 1, scheduled over time, that scales a route's or a population's rate: 0 shuts
it, 1 leaves it as declared. A drug that blocks a channel, or a treatment that opens it, is an
availability schedule.

- **In other fields:** the fraction not blocked (biology); an inhibition factor (chemistry); a
  time-dependent coefficient (physics); a control input, held between change times (computing).
- **Unit:** none (a fraction).
- **Cells' word;** see [the inner-model design](../INNER_MODEL_V4_DESIGN.md) (§7.4).

<!-- generated: used-by availability -->

- **Used by:** [`cells.sodium_calcium_exchange_symmetric_barrier@1`](cells/laws/sodium_calcium_exchange_symmetric_barrier@1.md) and [`cells.ideal_osmosis_linear_compliance_water@1`](cells/operators/ideal_osmosis_linear_compliance_water@1.md).

<!-- /generated -->

### Booking

Recording, in a piece's own accumulators, every amount and every energy it moves, so that the
ledgers can check the totals at each step.

- **In other fields:** a mass and energy balance (biology, chemistry); keeping a conserved
  quantity's account (physics); accumulating terms in state that a check reads (computing).
- **Cells' word;** see the operator contract's energy roles and [the core-seams
  design](../CORE_SEAMS_DESIGN.md), §6 (heat booking).

- **Used by:** no page yet.

### Buffer

Molecules that bind and release an ion, usually protons, so quickly that the free concentration
changes much less than the total does. Cells' rapid proton buffer treats this binding as
instantaneous.

- **In other fields:** intracellular buffering (biology); an acid–base equilibrium (chemistry); a
  fast variable eliminated by its equilibrium (physics); a closure applied at every stage
  (computing).

### Channel

- **In other fields:** an ion channel (biology); a selective pore (chemistry); a conductance in

### Compliance

How much a compartment's volume grows when the pressure in it rises: its elastic give. Cells' water
operator uses a linear law, in which a stiffness sets the pressure each fractional stretch needs.

- **In other fields:** cell-wall or cortex elasticity (biology); a bulk modulus (chemistry); a
  linear spring between pressure and volume (physics); one parameter of the volume update
  (computing).
- **Unit:** the stiffness is in Pa.

<!-- generated: used-by compliance -->

### Constant-field flux

The flow of an ion through a membrane on the assumption that the electric field is uniform across
it (the Goldman–Hodgkin–Katz flux). It gives how the flow depends on the voltage and on the
concentrations on both sides.

- **In other fields:** the GHK current equation (biology); electrodiffusion through a thin layer
  (chemistry); the Nernst–Planck equation, integrated at constant field (physics); a closed-form
  rate kernel (computing).
- **Unit:** fmol/s for a population's flux.

<!-- generated: used-by constant-field-flux -->

- **Used by:** [`cells.kv1p5_mh@1`](cells/laws/kv1p5_mh@1.md).

<!-- /generated -->

### Cycle free energy

The free-energy change of one complete cycle of a pump or transporter: the fuel's drive plus the
cost of moving its cargo. Cells writes it $\Delta G_\text{cyc}$. A cycle runs forward only where it
is negative.

- **In other fields:** the energy balance of active transport (biology); the Gibbs energy of the
  coupled reaction (chemistry); the affinity of a cycle (physics); a value each stage computes and
  checks (computing).
- **Unit:** J/mol.

<!-- generated: used-by cycle-free-energy -->

- **Used by:** [`cells.transporter@1`](cells/kinds/transporter@1.md) and [`cells.sodium_calcium_exchange_symmetric_barrier@1`](cells/laws/sodium_calcium_exchange_symmetric_barrier@1.md).

<!-- /generated -->

### Definition

The JSON document a run is made from: its model (with every value), its experiment (times and
schedules) and its execution (solver and step). Definitions hold values; models declare structure.

- **In other fields:** an experimental protocol (biology); a set of initial and boundary conditions
  (chemistry, physics); a configuration file, parsed and checked before anything runs (computing).
- **Cells' word;** see [the architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

### Domain

The range of inputs where a law is valid, such as an interval of voltages. A step outside it is
refused, never clipped.

- **In other fields:** the conditions a measurement covered (biology); the range of a fit
  (chemistry); the validity range of an approximation (physics); a precondition checked at run
  time (computing).
- **Cells' word;** see [the architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

<!-- generated: used-by domain -->

- **Used by:** no page yet.

<!-- /generated -->

### Electrochemical potential

The free energy per mole of an ion where it is, from its concentration and from its charge in the
local voltage together. Ions left to themselves flow from higher to lower.

- **In other fields:** the driving force on an ion (biology); the electrochemical potential
  (chemistry, physics); a quantity computed per species and node (computing).
- **Unit:** J/mol.

<!-- generated: used-by electrochemical-potential -->

- **Used by:** no page yet.

<!-- /generated -->

### fmol

### Gate

The part of a channel that opens or shuts it. For each gate, Cells tracks the fraction of a
population's channels in which that gate is open: a number from 0 to 1.

- **In other fields:** an activation or inactivation gate (biology); a two-state conformational
  equilibrium (chemistry); a relaxation variable (physics); a state variable advanced at each step
  (computing).
- **Unit:** none (a fraction).

<!-- generated: used-by gate -->

- **Used by:** [`cells.kv1p5_mh@1`](cells/laws/kv1p5_mh@1.md).

<!-- /generated -->

### Heat

Energy that a process dissipates, which is never negative for a passive one. Cells books each
piece's heat, so that the energy ledger closes.

- **In other fields:** metabolic heat (biology); dissipation, $-T\Delta S$ of the surroundings
  (chemistry); entropy production times temperature (physics); an accumulator a check reads
  (computing).
- **Unit:** pJ.

<!-- generated: used-by heat -->

- **Used by:** [`cells.transporter@1`](cells/kinds/transporter@1.md) and [`cells.sodium_calcium_exchange_symmetric_barrier@1`](cells/laws/sodium_calcium_exchange_symmetric_barrier@1.md).

<!-- /generated -->

### Held bath

An outside space whose volume and composition are held fixed, standing in for a large reservoir.
What flows into or out of it is booked as supply, with its work.

- **In other fields:** the bathing medium (biology); a reservoir at fixed concentrations
  (chemistry); a boundary held by a thermostat and a chemostat (physics); a node whose amounts are
  not state (computing).
- **Cells' word;** see [the water guide](../PUMP_WATER_MODEL.md).

<!-- generated: used-by held-bath -->

### Host

- **In other fields:** the model that uses a rate law (biology, chemistry); the system a
  constitutive law closes (physics); the caller that owns the state a pure function reads

- **Cells' word,** defined in [the format](DECLARATIONS_FORMAT.md#the-id-rule).

### Hydraulic permeability

Aquaporins raise it.

- **In other fields:** water permeability (biology); a membrane's filtration coefficient

  (computing).
- **Unit:** m/(s Pa).

### Membrane voltage

- **Unit:** V; inside less outside.

### Refusal
