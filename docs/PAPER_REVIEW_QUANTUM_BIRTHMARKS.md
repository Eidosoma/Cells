**Quantum birthmarks: implications for Cells — reviewed 11 October 2026**

Prompting article: Ingrid Fadelli, [*Quantum 'birthmarks' hold lasting traces of a
system's past*](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-quantum-birthmarks.html), Phys.org,
11 October 2026. Primary source: Graf, Atwood, Xiao, Ketzmerick, Heller and
Keski-Rahkonen, [*Quantum Birthmarks: Ergodicity Breaking Beyond
Scarring*](https://doi.org/10.1103/dhzb-28rb), Physical Review X **16**, 031063,
10 September 2026; [author manuscript v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02982v4).

**Relevant as inspiration for testing retained history, identifying its physical
carriers, and evaluating reduced models.** For Cells, the useful question is
whether preparations that look similar now retain differences that predict later
behaviour. This fits *Plastic Heredity*, *How Forms Become Reconstructible* and
the planned two-task assay. The experiments below are proposals, with no assumed
positive outcome.

The primary evidence is theory plus stadium-billiard wave-packet simulations.
Under isolated unitary dynamics, the authors describe enhanced long-time return
to an initial state and its early trajectory, relative to matched reference
states. They separate a symmetry-dependent contribution from additional
enhancement associated with early recurrences. Spectral constraints matter to
the reference comparison. These are quantum calculations, not new biological
measurements. See [PRX, Sections II–III](https://journals.aps.org/prx/pdf/10.1103/dhzb-28rb).

Two qualifications sharpen the news account: the paper associates its exploration
limit with **Heisenberg time**, while Thouless time enters a different cutoff
discussion; Section IV A explicitly says the result does **not invalidate
eigenstate thermalization**. Environmental decoherence remains an extension to
investigate. Supporting data are available on request, rather than deposited
publicly. See [Sections II A/C, IV A/C and Data
Availability](https://journals.aps.org/prx/pdf/10.1103/dhzb-28rb).

The separate [Xiaoya et al. preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00891v1),
*Universal Quantum Birthmark: Ghost of the quantum past*, develops the
symmetry/dimension contribution. Only its abstract and version metadata were
reviewed here. The main-paper review covered definitions, time scales, selected
simulation results, interpretation and data availability; supplementary methods
were not audited, and no result was reproduced.

The proposed Cells connection is methodological. Our ionic and stochastic
assembly models do not propagate quantum amplitudes. A durable classical
signature needs an identifiable state, structure, environment or dynamical
mechanism. Finite simulated persistence alone cannot establish permanent memory,
nonergodicity, biological use or heredity. Keep the existing distinctions between
[retention, transmission and renewal](SCIENTIFIC_BASIS.md#operational-distinctions-to-retain).

| Future question | Proposed Cells comparison | What would count as useful evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does history remain detectable after visible recovery? | Prepare different cue histories, remove the cue, match declared present observables and apply a common future challenge. | Predictive differences on held-out preparations, beyond matched present-state and input-only baselines. |
| Where is the retained information? | Reset, exchange or disable a candidate gate, contact, chemical store or assembly structure; include sham and resource-matched controls. | Selective loss and rescue of the response, with valid charge/material accounting. |
| Does early recurrence predict later retention? | Relate early return statistics to later responses across independent worlds, then manipulate a specified feedback route. | Prediction that survives matching and changes under the causal intervention; early/late correlation alone is insufficient. |
| Is the information usable and reconstructible? | Give an explicit reader limited access, then test transfer and later carrier replacement when those mechanisms exist. | Reader-dependent performance and repeated reconstruction, measured separately from analyst decoding and checkpoint copying. |
| What does coarse graining erase? | Compare reductions retaining only mean voltage or assembly size with reductions also retaining spatial patterns, gates or topology. | Agreement on held-out trajectories and interventions for both tasks; label agreement alone is insufficient. |

A small first extension can reuse
[E04: matched phenotype, different history](EXPERIMENTS.md#e04-extension-matched-phenotype-different-history)
and the [planned polycomputing readouts](MULTISCALE_EXECUTION_ARCHITECTURE.md#evidence-to-seek-in-cells).
Keep the current BETSE qualification and initial two-task assay ahead of this
extension. Use one qualified model family and a horizon appropriate to its
verified domain; the present microsecond comparison panels do not establish
biological memory timescales.

1. Define two preparation histories and a cue-free interval. Freeze the matching
   variables, tolerances, observation clock and challenge before evaluation.
   Match geometry, parameters, reservoirs and relevant conserved inventories;
   report residual differences instead of asserting complete-state equality.
2. Compare future responses and a bounded, dimensionless similarity of selected
   physical readouts. Measure both within-world persistence and similarity to
   other matched worlds at the same lag. Freeze feature scaling on calibration
   data. Use whole independent worlds/preparation families for held-out tests;
   neighbouring windows from one trajectory are not independent replicates.
3. Include unconditioned and common-endpoint controls, shuffled history labels
   within matched groups, longer cue-free intervals, altered observation windows,
   and timestep/precision checks. Estimate uncertainty across worlds. Report
   decay, censoring and null results; do not extrapolate a plateau to infinite
   time or import a quantum enhancement factor into a classical score.
4. Locate a candidate carrier using admissible ablation/rescue and a common
   challenge. A reset that changes ionic amounts, charge or assembly material
   must declare and account for that intervention. Identical complete
   deterministic states with identical future inputs must replay identically.
   For stochastic models, also specify pending events and paired versus
   independent random streams; distinct random realizations are not evidence
   for history-dependent physics.

**Keep the observer from supplying the answer.** Preparation labels, world IDs,
random keys, event counters, cumulative accounting ledgers and archived
trajectories belong to analysis/provenance unless an explicit physical reader
can access them. They must not become biological memory features by accident.
Preserve them in complete checkpoints for reproducibility. A decoder's own
history buffer can retain a cue after the tissue has forgotten it; compare with
an input-only reader and separately test readouts initialized after cue removal.
This extends the existing separation of physical and analysis state in the
[execution architecture](MULTISCALE_EXECUTION_ARCHITECTURE.md#preserve-the-information-needed-to-test-the-claim).

Assembly is a useful later candidate because equal subunit counts can conceal
different bond configurations. Compare size-only observations with topology and
their effects on coupled ionic responses. Control for the supplied rules: the
original [capsid model](CAPSID_ASSEMBLY.md#model-and-scope) protects loops and has
an absorbing complete cage. Persistence imposed by those rules cannot establish
an acquired, renewable carrier. Use the
[finite-material, breakable-bond family](CAPSID_ASSEMBLY.md#finite-material-and-breakable-bonds)
for turnover questions, and keep any feedback into assembly rates explicitly
modelled and independently checked.

For CPU/L4 and later Jupiter work, implement these as bounded read-only summaries
over accepted states, with saved observer buffers and cadence-independent replay.
Avoid retaining every pairwise comparison across all worlds and times. Batch
comparable preparation/challenge arms through the existing ensemble machinery,
retain stable world identities, and record both GPU busy and measured SM-active
with coverage. The first step is an observer/assay extension, not a change to the
physical equations. [E09/E11/E12](EXPERIMENTS.md#later-experiments) supply the
later carrier, reader and reduction tests.
