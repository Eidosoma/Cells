This reconciles the user's acceptance targets for FR-45 (`docs/FEATURE_REQUESTS.md`, FR-45's
section, commit 0b68722c) with the registration, before the tag and before any engine run. It is
value-silent: it states no result of any world. The amounts below are the user's example, and the
exact consequences of SPEC.md §1.4's map on it, not results.

- **The criteria they enter are named here** (§3), and SPEC.md, CRITERIA.md and the protocol
