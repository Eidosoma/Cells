**Each ruling is one of three kinds,** as for FR-43 part 3:

- **value:** a registered value or fact reproduces one reading only.
- **open:** the text allows both readings. The reading taken is the one that cannot make a pass easier.

No ruling changes a criterion, band, plant or tolerance (CRITERIA:3, 66; PROTOCOL:101).

## 1. Two rules that several rulings rest on

(CRITERIA:39-41, 66, 113-114; PROTOCOL:131-133)
- **On an honest run,** a criterion passes or fails, and inconclusive arises only from the references' convergence.
  So an entry that is missing, cannot be formed, or is touched by a contract fault cannot pass, and its criterion fails.
  FR-43 part 3 differs: its text had an inconclusive family.
- **On a plant run,** that same entry is **not caught**.
- Each is the reading that cannot make a pass easier. A fault that failed a criterion on a plant run would catch the
  plant, which is the easier reading there (the review's B1).
- **The verdict keys of `agreement.py`:**
  - An honest pair passes only where `passes` is true, so a NaN or an infinity fails it. (`judge_derived` gives a NaN
    both `passes` and `fails` false.)
  - A plant entry fails only where `fails` is true.
  - A plant entry whose engine value is not finite cannot be formed, so it is not caught, whatever `judge_constant`
    returns.

## 5. What a builder may see (amends RULINGS-1's list)

- **Before any engine run, the builder receives:**
  - the registration's seven files;
  - RULINGS-1 and these rulings;
  - the final contract, its format sample and the expected-inputs file;
  - the readings' text;
  - the census;
  - the references' format note, `REFERENCE-FORMAT-NOTE-2026-11-27.txt`. This is the writer's module docstring
    (NOTES:210), extracted verbatim from the tagged blob. It defines every key the files hold, `against`, `scale`,
    `minimum`, `spans_wash` and `dt_s` among them. It holds no value, as the lead checked, and the lead hashes it;
  - the registered `agreement.py`;
  - the references' invented-input file, `format_sample_INVENTED.json`.
- **The invented file holds no registered result.** SPEC:391-392 has the judge read it before any engine run, and it
  shares no trajectory, equilibrium, heat or plant value with any registered file. Of its 466 floats, 45 also appear in
  a registered file:
  - 3 are 0, 1, 0.5, 2 or −1;
  - 14 sit only at input or time keys;
  - 22 are rounding-level, below 10⁻¹²;
  - 6 equal a registered input.
- **The references:** the builder imports `G` as NOTES:176-186 shows, and never reads the references' modules' source.
- **Never, before the judged run:** the references' registered outputs (`outputs/`), or the stopped judge's notes or
  scripts.
