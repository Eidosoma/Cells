# FR-45's spec: amendment 3 of 24 November 2026, before the tag (the references agent's three findings on W7 and W7s)

This rules on the references agent's three findings on amendment 2's worlds, before the tag and
before any engine run.
- **It is value-silent:** it states no result of any world.
- **No band changes,** and no plant's mechanism.
- **It corrects two of amendment 2's inputs and fixes one key.** SPEC.md, CRITERIA.md and the
  protocol follow, each marked with this amendment.

## 1. W7s's horizon is shortened to 2.0 s, 47 time constants

**The finding.**
- **The horizon was oversized.** W7s's 12 s horizon was 280 time constants of its slowest mode
  (23.3 /s, amendment 2 §2), against the protocol's sizing of at least 30.
- **Its late heats fell below the references' resolution:**
  - the 3–5 s heat kept about 7 significant digits;
  - the 5–8 s and 8–12 s heats were the 50-digit integration's rounding noise, one of them
    negative.
- **No criterion could see this,** but a registered reference entry must carry its value.

**The ruling.**
- **W7s's horizon is 2.0 s,** 46.6 time constants: the protocol's sizing of at least 30, with margin.
  - Its observation times are 0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 s.
  - Near the root an interval's heat falls as the square of the distance from it. So the last
    interval, 1.5 to 2.0 s, stays far above the references' resolution.
- **The sizing rule gains its upper side.** A horizon reaches at least 30 time constants of its
  slowest mode. It must not go so far beyond that, though, that an interval's heat falls below
  the references' resolution.
- **W7 is unchanged:**
  - its 11 s after the wash is 52 time constants;
  - its last interval's heat is resolved;
  - the agent reports no issue there.

## 2. The step bound is dt·max L ≤ 0.03, as registered: W7 and W7s step at 0.0025 s

**The finding.**
- **What is registered:** the protocol's §4 bounds dt·max L by 0.03 for every world, and the
  registered worlds sit at 0.023, 0.0075, 0.030 and 0.020.
- **Amendment 2's error:** it wrote h·max L = 0.03 for W7 and W7s, "as for the registered
  worlds", confusing h = dt/2 with dt. At dt = 0.005 s and max L = 12 /s (matrix_L1's k_off),
  dt·max L was 0.06, twice the registered bound.

**The ruling.**
- **The bounded quantity is dt·max L, at 0.03,** as §4 registers. Amendment 2's sentence is
  withdrawn.
- **W7's and W7s's dt is 0.0025 s,** so dt·max L = 0.03. No reference value depends on dt.

**What follows.**
- **W7's first row after its wash moves from 1.005 s to 1.0025 s.**
  - Its observation times are 0.5, 1.0, 1.0025, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0, 5.0, 8.0 and 12.0 s.
  - The wash's excluded heat interval (C3a, C3b) is 1.0 to 1.0025 s.
- **W7s's observation times** are §1's.
- **The dry run,** on neighbouring inputs at the amended step and W7s's horizon (probe
  `w7_amendment3_dry_run.py`, beside amendment 2's):
  - W7: C1 at 0.008 of its band, C2a at 0.005;
  - W7s: smaller.

## 3. P5's C6 entries on W7: two, one per state

**The finding.**
- **The references wrote two entries for P5 under C6 on W7,** one for each state the planted map is
  applied to: 0.0 and 1.0 s.
- **SPEC.md §3.5 asks for one entry per (plant, criterion) pair.** A judge indexing by that pair
  would collide, and P5 also runs on W2.

**The ruling: two entries, one per state.**
- **Every plant entry is keyed (plant, criterion, world, time_s):**
  - time_s is the state's time for a plant applied to a saved state: 4.0 s for P5 on W2, and 0.0
    and 1.0 s on W7;
  - time_s is null for a plant judged over a run.
- **There is one entry per key.** As §3.5 asks, each names its compared quantity of largest
  distance.
- **The judge indexes by the key.**

**Why two.** The example holds still until the wash. A single entry would tie at 0.0 s and hide
1.0 s, the state the wash acts on.
