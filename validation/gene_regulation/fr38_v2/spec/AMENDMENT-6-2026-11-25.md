# Amendment 6, 25 November 2026: a fail is never hidden, and the placement and tag of the registered runs

**Written by the registration's author at the lead's request, before the judge's tag.**
- **Bands.** It changes no band. Every criterion, tolerance, accuracy requirement and plant stands, as
  amendments 1 to 5 left them. Item 3 narrows what an engine-only equality covers, as it says there.
- **Value-silent.** It states no reference, engine or sample value.
- **Precedence.** Where it and the earlier files of this folder differ, it governs. Items 7 and 8 are
  process lines for the protocol's §6; they touch no band and no value.

## 1. A refusal never hides a fail

**The rule.**
1. **Every entry that can be formed is judged.** An entry can be formed when the runs, rungs and
   quantities it reads are present and not refused.
2. **If any judged entry of a run fails, the run fails** (CRITERIA.md §4: any C1 or C4 entry, or a C2 entry
   whose readout's order is inside its window or unresolved), whatever else of that run, or of its support
   runs, is refused or missing.
3. **With no failing entry, a run any of whose own rungs is refused or missing is scored refused,** and
   cannot pass. A C0 refusal of a rung's entry (a definition, a missing output or readout, a non-finite
   value, a pinned key) is a refusal of that rung. For a run that takes the retry, these are the retry's
   rungs.
4. **A refused or missing support run leaves its arm or family at best inconclusive.** Support runs are
   the blank, the twin and AN's restart. It does not by itself refuse the labelled run, and the arm rule's
   "any run" counts it (CRITERIA.md §4).
5. **A refused run takes no retry** (CRITERIA.md §1: the retry is for inconclusive runs only).

**What can be formed.**
- A stepped entry (a C2 entry other than route 2) needs its three rungs. Route 2 is judged at h alone
  (CRITERIA.md C2), so it needs only h. Where a rung is refused or missing, only the entries that need
  it are not formed; C1 and C4 entries, and route 2's at h, are still judged at the rungs that ran.
- **A table entry that C0 refuses forms no judged entry at its rung.** Under item 2 this includes a
  recorded-only leaf that is missing, mistyped or non-finite. So a wrong value at that rung can still
  sit behind such a refusal. This is the one place where a refusal of a leaf that no criterion reads
  leaves the rung's judged readouts unjudged. It follows from the ruling in item 2, and is kept: an entry
  C0 refuses for a malformed leaf is a corrupt file, not a judged result, and the checker sees the refusal
  and its cause. The lead reports that none of the 122 invented-world files holds one.
- An equality with a twin, the restart or AF's other blank is formed only when both runs are present.
- `excess_over_blank` is formed only where the run's blank is present and not refused, at each rung it
  needs. Where it is not, the table writer leaves the key out (it is absent, not null), the run's `blank`
  still holds the blank's name, and the entry survives with everything else. **Absent is not a fault where
  its blank is refused or missing,** and amendment 5, §2 point 4's pinned-key rule yields to this, for the
  run and for its twin. The run's other entries are judged.

**The plants.** Amendment 4, item 7's "a refused honest run makes the plant unscorable" means the honest
run's own entries. A refused twin, blank or restart alone does not make a plant unscorable.
- **P1″ is the exception, as the lead confirms:** it compares the engine's `excess_over_blank`, so it
  needs the blank. Where the blank is refused or missing, P1″ is unscorable: neither caught nor
  "not caught", and FR-38's comparison is at best inconclusive. In AN that is the family, which has one
  blank. In AF each machine option has its own blank, so a refused one makes unscorable only that
  option's entries, in the must-catch groups that rest on them (each idle fate on its own). A P1″ that is
  judged and not caught elsewhere is still a fail: an unscorable group hides no other group.
- **P8** does not depend on its blank for its catch: `n_M`, `n_Mx` and the labels do not read the blank,
  so a refused `P8 blank` leaves P8 scorable on them.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes.** An entry that can be formed and fails is never read as inconclusive
or refused. A refusal, or a refused or missing support run, never turns a pass into a fail, and can turn a
pass into inconclusive. The one place a fail can still go unseen is the C0-refused entry named above.

## 2. Recorded-only leaves

- **"Recorded, never judged" exempts a value from a criterion, not from being present with its type and
  finite.**
- **The leaves** are `checks`, `own_ledgers_at_end`, route 2's `grid_error` and `k_per_s`, `blank` and
  `host_patch`. **The faults,** as the judge reads them: a pinned leaf (amendment 5, §2 point 4) that is
  missing or of another type refuses its entry. A `blank` that is null or of another type where a string
  is required, and a `host_patch` that is not null on a plant other than P9, are such leaves of the wrong
  type.
- A blank's `route2` and `survival` are excepted there from being required. Where present they must have
  their registered types.
- **A non-finite value anywhere in an entry refuses it** (CRITERIA.md C0).
- **The lead reports,** value-free, that 122 files the builder's real table code wrote on invented worlds
  hold no NaN and no infinity, and null at a number's place only at a survival ratio. (In the registered
  sample, null stands only there and at the optional `blank` and `host_patch`; the definition has nulls of
  its own.)
- **Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** it can refuse an entry, and so leave an arm at best inconclusive.
  It cannot turn a fail into a pass.

## 3. What the equalities of C1 cover: narrowed, and said plainly

CRITERIA.md §2 registers three engine-only equalities (a labelled run and its twin, the resumed run and
the uninterrupted one, and AF's two blanks). They run over these named things only:

| Equality | Over |
| --- | --- |
| A labelled run and its twin | READOUTS.md §1's series, except the ten labels and `released_present_gross` (and so the survival readout); route 2's values; the excess over the blank; the engine's `checks` |
| AN's restart and the uninterrupted run | READOUTS.md §1's series, the ten labels and `released_present_gross` included; route 2's values; `survival`; the excess over the blank; the engine's `checks` |
| AF's two blanks | READOUTS.md §1's series, labels and `released_present_gross` included |

- **Outside these,** engine_run's other exports (for example `H_route_*`, `int_D_route_*`, `H_total`,
  `H_routes_sum`, `n_tx_release_*`, `label_H_*`; `n_K` and `n_Y` in D; `W_network` in C), `grid_error` and
  `k_per_s`, and `own_ledgers_at_end` are recorded only. A difference in one is **flagged to the checker
  and reported beside the verdict,** and fails nothing by itself.
- **This narrows amendment 4, item 7,** whose "every series" (twin) and "every record" (restart), read
  literally, would include them, and CRITERIA.md §2's "physical readouts" and "records" likewise. **Why:**
  amendment 5, item 4 makes engine_run's other exports recorded, never judged, and where the two differ
  amendment 5 governs. The narrowing is made here, before any registered run, and disclosed as a
  narrowing. The labels' physical promise (FR-42's) is still judged bit for bit on every named series.
- **Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** an unequal named thing fails its run (C1). Not a recorded-only one.

## 4. P9 refused by the engine

- **A refused P9 is caught,** where its run is refused by the engine's own books or checks (CRITERIA.md
  §4; amendment 4, item 7).
- A refused entry has exactly four keys (amendment 5, item 5), so item 7's `host_patch` requirement cannot
  apply to it: its patch cannot be read from the table.
- **The checker confirms,** from the run's own record, that the V₀ patch was on, and that the refusal is the
  engine's own and not a failure of the harness. If the patch was not on, or the refusal is not the
  engine's own, P9 is unscorable.
- If D's honest `p_sched returned` is itself refused, P9 is unscorable (amendment 4, item 7).
- This item settles P9 alone. The general rule for a refused plant (CRITERIA.md §4; amendment 4, item 7)
  stands as it is.
- **Can a verdict depend on it? Family D's verdict only,** and never FR-38's.

## 5. AN's restart null or absent

- **It refuses the restart alone** (amendment 5, item 5 and §4's list), never AN's whole table. AN's
  first-pass table is the one that holds `restart`; the retry's tables hold none (amendment 5).
  Amendment 5, §2 point 4's general rule for a missing top-level key yields to the specific one for
  `restart`.
- By §1 point 4, a refused or missing restart leaves AN at best inconclusive, and does not refuse AN's
  other runs.
- **Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** arm A at best inconclusive. Never a fail.

## 7. Placement (the protocol's §6, step 7)

- The line "on the light CPUs, one thread each" becomes **"on CPUs the lead allots, one thread each, on one
  machine, stated in the record".**
- **Placement decides no value.** The machine and its libraries are one, and each run is one-threaded, cold

## 8. The registration tag (the protocol's §6, step 6)

- **The tag** `fr38-v2-registered-2026-11-25` is placed at a merge of main with `fr38-registration`,
  `fr38-v2-build` and `fr38-v2-judge`, after the judge conforms to this amendment.
- **The registered engine is the merged head's,** not the builder's older base ff9a410e. Main's engine has
  since gained the landings of FR-44, FR-47, FR-50 and FR-51 (18 `src` files).
- **The runs are made from a clean frozen snapshot of the registration tag** (the protocol's step 7). Their
  `source` names the snapshot and its engine revision. Amendment 5 asks C0 only for that form and a clean
  checkout; the checker compares them with the tag's.
- **Before the tag the lead re-runs, at that merge:** the builder's own checks (its test file,
  `table_check`, the parse check and the smoke on invented worlds), the judge's tests and the dry C0. The
  lead reports a clean trial merge of the three branches with main (49 files, no conflict).
- **Two further checks, at the merged head, before the tag:**
  - **The arm-A probe,** on made-up values at the three steps of the protocol's disclosures. Its observed
    orders must lie inside their windows, and its engine-only tolerances within CRITERIA.md §6's
    expectations for the readouts it measures (labels and `released_present_gross`, amounts, the hold and
    the releases, `G_minus_G0`, and the heats). They were measured on main's engine of 24 November
    (cd3b7b09), and main's engine has changed since.
  - **The format sample,** regenerated at the merged head and compared, key by key and type by type, with
    the registered sample's README (amendment 5).
- **If either differs,** the lead returns to this author for a dated amendment before the tag, never after.
  For the probe, "differs" is an order outside its window or a tolerance above its expectation; it is
  settled as CRITERIA.md §6 settles a plant below its 10×. For the sample, it is any key, nesting or type
  that departs.
- **No engine run of a registered world has been made at either base,** as the lead states.
- **Can a verdict depend on item 7? No.** On item 8, only in naming the engine that is judged: the verdict
  is that head's. No registered run exists at either base, so none can be traced to one.
