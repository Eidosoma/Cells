# Amendment 4, 24 November 2026: the judge's questions, and the engine export contract

**Written by the registration's author at the lead's request, before the judge's tag.**
- **Bands.** It changes no band. Every criterion, tolerance, accuracy requirement and plant stands,
  as amendments 1 to 3 left them.
- **Value-silent.** It states no reference or engine value.
- **Precedence.** Where it and the earlier files of this folder differ, it governs.
- **What its author read:**
  - the judge's eight questions as the lead relayed them, in words. They include one figure about
    arm B's reference file, the size of item 1's scale difference. The judge's code was not opened.
  - the builder's README on branch `fr38-v2-build` at ac6b9204
    (`validation/gene_regulation/fr38_v2/README.md`), for item 8's contract. That README includes
    the builder's arithmetic for family D's swelling on the registered inputs, and its smoke figures

  - the run names and the plant list in the builder's world module at the same head.

  Its author opened no reference file and no engine record of a registered world.

## 1. G's scale in arm B: every chain's residues in transit

- **The intended reading is the sheet's §3.4.** G carries the standard term of every chain's
  residues in transit, each at its own residue potential, plus the hold's term. That is the G the
  engine side exports.
- **LAWS.md §5's formula** names only transcription's term, μ°_res (W_tx + L H). It was written for
  the hold worlds, which have one chain.
- **For every world** the formula reads Σ_chains μ°_res,ρ W_ρ + μ°_res,tx L H. READOUTS.md §3's G
  scale reads Σ_chains RT |μ°_res,ρ| W_ρ + RT |μ°_res,tx| L H in the same way.
  - In B the chains are `tx` (R's residue), and `tl` and `tl_s` (P's residue, amendment 1, item 6).
  - The hold worlds are unchanged.
- **Can a verdict depend on it? No.**
  - G is reported in B, not judged, so its scale enters no judged tolerance and no accuracy
    requirement.
  - C0 checks a stated scale only where a judged entry's accuracy requirement rests on it. A
    reported entry's stated scale that departs from READOUTS.md §3 is recorded, not refused.
- **Disclosed:** the judge chose this reading with B's reference file in view, which it has
  disclosed. The ruling rests on the sheet's §3.4 and on READOUTS.md §3's own principle, the
  magnitude of the terms a quantity is formed from.

## 2. C4 at every rung: yes

- C4's identities close at rounding at any step. So they are checked at every rung that runs (2, 1
  and 0.5 s, and a retry's 1, 0.5 and 0.25 s), each with that rung's own step count S.
- **The exception** is family D's energy identity, which amendment 3 made a stepped entry: C2 at h,
  with C3 over the three rungs.
- **Can a verdict depend on it? Yes.** A C4 failure at any rung fails its run (CRITERIA.md §4).

## 3. Blanks are judged runs: yes

- Each engine blank is a run of its arm (AF, AN) or family (C, D).
- It is judged by C1 and C2 against the reference's one `blank`, at every quantity the reference
  publishes for it, as any run is.
- AF's two engine blanks must also be equal bit for bit (C1; CRITERIA.md §2).
- **Can a verdict depend on it? Yes.** A failed blank fails its arm or family.

## 4. P8's negative control: the literal text is intended

- A failed negative control, with P8 caught and C's runs passing, leaves family C inconclusive, not
  failed (CRITERIA.md §4).
- **Why:** the negative control tests that the plant acted only where predicted. Its failure means
  the plant's evidence for the gross classes' independence of M is not established. It does not
  overturn the honest runs, which passed.
- **Can a verdict depend on it?** Family C's verdict, yes. FR-38's verdict, no: family C does not
  decide it.

## 5. The judge's own plants: yes

- **P1 and P1″** are mapping plants, **P1′** is a host-side form, and **P6** is a record plant. None
  needs an engine run.
  - The judge forms each from the honest records at h, by the spec's definitions.
  - **P1′:** LAWS.md §6's net-rate form on the engine's own counters.
  - **P6:** the honest G − G(0) less RT μ°_res L (H − H(0)), with μ°_res as amendment 1, item 6
    forms it.
- **The world-data plants** come from the builder's runs: P2, P3a, P3b, P4 (AN and B), P5, P7, P8
  with its planted blank, and P9.
- **A builder export entry that presents itself as P1, P1′, P1″ or P6** is excluded from scoring
  and recorded. It does not refuse the rest of the export. The builder's table may drop such
  entries (item 8).
- **Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** catches enter the verdict. Only the judge's own formation
  counts.

## 6. A missing manifest: recorded, not refused

- **Completeness** is judged against the registered run list: CRITERIA.md §1, with P8's planted
  blank (amendment 1, item 4) and item 8's names.
- **A missing index** (a manifest, or the table's `SHA256SUMS`) is recorded in the verdict and
  flagged to the checker. It is not refused, as long as every registered run's own record is
  present with its provenance and its `record_sha256`.
- **A missing run, or a run without its provenance,** is refused (C0).
- **The table's sha256,** taken before any reference value is read (the protocol's step 8), is the
  lead's record, which the checker verifies.
- **Can a verdict depend on it?** Not on the index alone.

## 8. The engine export contract: registered here

**Yes, the spec states it,** so that the builder and the judge each read only this folder.
- It is the engine table that the protocol hashes before any reference value is read (the
  protocol's step 8), as the builder's README at ac6b9204 documents it.
- The judge's reader conforms to it before its tag.
- A key the judge needs that is not here is requested through the lead and added by a dated
  amendment, never taken from the other side's code.
- Extra keys are allowed, and the reader ignores them (amendment 2, Q6).

### 8.1 Files

- **One JSON file per family:** `table/table_<family>.json`, for AF, AN, B, C and D, with their
  sha256 in `table/SHA256SUMS`. The retry's go in `table-retry/`.
- **The tables** are built from the engine's run records alone. Each run's record is engine_run's
  JSON file, named in its entry with its sha256.

### 8.2 Top-level keys and run names

**Top-level keys:**
- `kind`: `"cells.fr38_v2.engine_table@1"`;
- `family`, `window_s`, `samples_s`;
- `missing`: the records not found;
- `rungs`: keyed by the step's `repr` (`"2.0"`, `"1.0"`, `"0.5"`; in the retry `"1.0"`, `"0.5"`,
  `"0.25"`). Each holds `dt_s` and `runs`.

**Run names:**
- the labelled runs: SPEC.md §1.3's names, and B's `p=0.55 returned` and `p=0.55 held`;
- the blanks: `blank returned` and `blank held` in AF, and `blank returned` in AN, C and D. Each
  is judged against the reference's one `blank` (item 3);
- a twin: its labelled run's name followed by ` unlabelled`.

### 8.3 A run's entry

A run's entry is either a refusal (`refused`, `refusal`, `definition`) or holds:
- `t`: its output times;
- `readouts`: READOUTS.md §1's series by name;
  - in labelled runs, `released_present_gross_<fate>` too;
  - engine_run's other exports, which READOUTS.md §4 leaves uncompared;
- `route2`: per fate and sample time, `value` (the known-rate form at the definition's k_f) and
  `grid_error`. The judge forms its own g(ts) (item 7);
- `survival`, in labelled runs: per fate and later sample time, `alive_t2`, `alive_ts` and `ratio`.
  The ratio is null at a zero count;
- `excess_over_blank`, in runs with a blank: per fate, one value per output, with `blank`, the
  matched blank's name;
- `checks`, `own_ledgers_at_end`, `steps`, `dt_s`, `definition`, `source`, and `record` with its
  `record_sha256`.

### 8.4 The restart and the plants

- **`restart`** (AN): the resumed run's record in the same form, with the checkpoint's identity.
- **`plants`,** at rung `"0.5"`: each world-data plant's record in the same form, with `judged_as`,
  the honest run it is judged against:
  - P2 (AF `p=1 returned`);
  - P3a and P3b (AN `p_sched returned`);
  - `P4 AN` and `P4 B` (the `returned` runs);
  - P5 (AF `p_sched returned`);
  - P7 (judged as AF `p_sched held`);
  - P8 and `P8 blank` (C `p_sched returned` and `blank`);
  - P9 (D `p_sched returned`, with the record naming its V₀ patch).
- Entries for P1, P1′, P1″ or P6 are ignored (item 5).
