## 1. Family D's energy identity (CRITERIA.md C4): corrected

**The registered text.** "In D the identity is G − G(0) + U(V) − U(V₀) + Σ heats − `W_bath` = 0,
with the same form of bound", the rounding form 8 ε S (E + …).

**Why it is wrong.**
- Water's bookings come from water's Heun half-steps, a second-order discrete scheme. They are its
  heat, the bath's work, and its dilution of every row's term.
- So family D's energy identity closes at O(dt²), not at rounding.
- The registration already declares order 2 for every family-D readout for that reason (CRITERIA.md
  §3, "water's Heun half-steps").
- FR-43's C2.7 judged its swelling cell's endpoint identity at second order, for the same reason.
- The rounding form was carried over from the fixed-volume worlds by mistake.

**The correction.** Family D's identity residual r(t), the left side above, is judged as a stepped
entry by C2 and C3. Its exact value is 0 (the law's), so e_ref = 0, and p = 2:
- **The step estimate.** With d₁ = max_t |r_2h − r_h| and d₂ = max_t |r_4h − r_2h|, the estimate is
  est = max(d₁/3, d₂/12).
- **The rounding.** e_round = 8 ε S_h s, with s the identity's own terms:
  - the bookings' magnitude, Σ_c |H_c| (`H_water` among them) plus |`W_bath`|;
  - every species row's G term, as amendment 1, item 7 reads family D's scale.
- **Pass:** max_t |r_h| ≤ 3 est + e_round.
- **The order (C3).** Where d₁ and d₂ both exceed 10 e_round, q = log₂(d₂/d₁) must lie in
  [1.5, 3.5]. Otherwise the run is inconclusive and takes the retry.
- **What it catches.**
  - A residual that does not shrink, from an error of order one in the bookings, has est near 0
    and fails.
  - One that shrinks only at first order is inconclusive by C3.

**Unchanged:**
- family D's other C4 identities: the membership sum and §9.7's, which are amount identities and
  close at rounding;
- the C4 of arms A and B and of family C, where each writer books the exact ΔG of its own moves, so
  the identity closes at rounding.

**Disclosed.** Before this correction, its author read the builder's figures on the builder's own
invented swelling cell (branch `fr38-v2-build`, ac6b9204):
- 2.64 × 10⁻⁵ pJ at dt 0.5 s and 6.59 × 10⁻⁶ pJ at 0.25 s;
- observed order 2.003;
- 5.1 × 10⁻⁸ of the bookings' magnitude.

The correction follows from the scheme's order, which the registration had already declared, not
from those figures. No registered world has run.

## 2. release_check's net-agreement scope: confirmed as intended

- **What the protocol asked** (§5): that the builder scope release_check's net-agreement sub-check
  to e^(y_f) ≪ 1, since it fails by design in AN.
- **The builder's form** (ac6b9204):
  - the sub-check applies where its reach, k_f (ts − t1) · max e^(y_f), is within SURVIVORS, the
    sub-check's own allowance;
  - e^(y_f) is formed from the record's own rows, potentials and volume;
  - elsewhere the sub-check is reported out of scope, neither pass nor fail;
  - a record that cannot form e^(y_f) stays in scope.
- **This is what the registration intends.**
  - The reach is the first-order bound on the relative gap between the net reading and the gross
    one. A member released at s ≥ t1 and read at ts differs by e^(k_f e^(y_f) (ts − s)) − 1.
  - R1's runs stay in scope.
  - AN falls out by design.
  - So does AF's short-lived fate at its later sample times, where the reach exceeds the allowance.
- **release_check is the builder's development acceptance, not a registered criterion.** The
  registration's discrimination of gross from net rests on C2 against the references (plants P1,
  P1′ and P1″), not on release_check.

## 3. Four readout controls for FR-38-type readouts, read against READOUTS.md and CRITERIA.md

**(a) A producer identified by cell and generation, two producers reordered, one replaced. Not
covered.**
- Producer identity across replacement (amendment G) is left to a later registration (D334; the
  protocol's §8). No registered world has a second producer or a replacement.
- **Development evidence exists, outside the registration.**
  - D306's independent checker ran two producers, in cells `cell_0` and `cell_1`, in both
    declaration orders, with `cell_0` replaced.
  - It found every amount, share, removal record and generation bitwise equal by cell ID
    (`/mnt/workspaces/Cells/review-check-20261123/runs/check5-reorder.json`). It used hand-written
    producer labels, before amendment G derived them.
  - `replace_restart.py`'s acceptance covers one producer, replaced and restarted, with the derived
    labels.
- **The mismatch is recorded:** a later registration.

**(b) Release-window survival, with membership by detachment from the template. Covered, and judged
in arms A and B.**
- **Membership** is the `win` class: the molecules released from the hold, that is detached from
  their template, during W = [t1, t2).
  - They are marked on transcription's own schedule at the start of each step.
  - Re-synthesised molecules are never members (erratum I, items 2–3; LAWS.md §1).
- **The denominator** is the `win` class at t2: the members still present at the window's end.
- **The numerator** is the same class at each later sample time: the same members still present
  (`survival_<fate>`, READOUTS.md §2; SPEC.md §3, item 4).
- **Judged** by C2, on the counts and on the ratio's tolerance. The ratio is undefined at a zero
  denominator (amendment 1, item 3).
- **Plant P4** marks the window at completion instead of at detachment, and must be caught in AN and
  in B (CRITERIA.md §6).

**(c) A reversible exchange, and how returns are counted.**
- **Covered for the one two-sided process on a fate row in these worlds, the degradation.**
  - Its forward part removes a molecule from its class for good.
  - Its backward part, re-synthesis from monomers, makes a new molecule. That molecule joins `resyn`
    and is never counted as a member's return (erratum I, items 1–2; LAWS.md §1).
  - No other route moves a labelled row here: family C's store exchanges the unlabelled monomer M,
    and family D's water moves only water.
- **Gross outward flow: no readout counts it.**
  - The fates' loss counters `int_D_*` are net (READOUTS.md §1).
  - No gross or one-way counter is exported (D334: no optional counters).
  - The gross reading enters only through membership (the labels) and through route 2's declared
    k_f.
- **Not covered:** an exchange in which the same molecule leaves and comes back.
  - That is the export across a patch (amendment T), with erratum H's two one-way parts: the forward
    part carries the cell's shares out, and the backward part carries the node's shares back.
  - It is left to a later registration (D334). The mismatch is recorded.

**(d) Zero loss, two simultaneous loss routes, a constant-stock control, and a resolution limit.**
- **Zero loss: not covered.** Every registered fate has k_f > 0. The mismatch is recorded.
- **Two simultaneous loss routes on one row: not covered.** Each registered fate row has one
  degradation. The mismatch is recorded.
  - `engine_run.py` sums every degradation route of a row at its cell into `int_D_<row>`, and route
    2's gross rate sums the row's declared rates.
  - Neither sum is exercised here.
- **A constant-stock control, with balanced production and loss: not covered.** No registered world
  is at steady state. The mismatch is recorded.
  - The host form's own control, `cohort.turnover_control`, checks the host calculation only, not
    the engine.
- **A resolution limit when sampling is too coarse: covered in the host form, and stated here for
  the registration.**
  - Route 2's tolerance carries its grid error g(ts) (CRITERIA.md C2). The judge reports g(ts) with
    each route-2 entry.
  - Where g(ts) cannot be estimated, because the window holds no output to drop, `cohort.py`
    returns no estimate rather than a zero. The route-2 entry is then scored refused (insufficient
    resolution), never a pass, and its run is at best inconclusive (CRITERIA.md §4).
  - It cannot occur at the registered grids: each window holds 120 outputs (A, C and D) or 60 (B).
    This clarifies a case the registered grids never reach, and changes no band.
- **The uncovered controls could enter only by a scope decision of the user,** as a further family
  verdicted on its own or in a later registration.
