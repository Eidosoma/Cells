## 1. Summary

- **M1. N2 alone gains nothing over today's admitted step on any gated sheet.** On every sheet
  with E07's gated junctions, the step is already held at 5.18 ms by the junction-gate bound,
  not by charge relaxation. N2 removes the membrane and junction charge terms but not that
  bound. At 825 nodes, face diffusion binds first, at 3.45 ms, and N2 does not touch it either.
- **M2. The sheets are well separated.** ε is 1.4–1.5 × 10⁻³ on the default cell and both
  seven-cell sheets. The slow spectrum would let an explicit slow integrator take about 170–190 s,
  3.2–3.5 × 10⁴ times N2's step on the sheets.
- **The harness is H2:** projective qual-01's F1 world at a 3600 s horizon. Its 0.3 s cue at
  1800 s switches cell_0 from the H branch to D for good.
  - All three arms ran, at 1, 0.5 and 0.25 ms, and every accepted row passed the engine's checks.
  - **Neither the 1 ms nor the 0.5 ms run meets every registered reference-adequacy budget,** so
    stage 1's reference is the 0.25 ms run, with its error unestimated (§4.2).
  - Every worst error lies in the switch transient: 2.97 µV and 6.3 × 10⁻⁵ in gate at 1 ms,
    within 0.15 of the voltage cap. In the slow phases the 1 ms error is below 1 nV.
  - The registered observed order is 1.3–1.5, just below the window, because it is measured
    in the switch's wake. Before the cue the order is about 2.3.
- **M3. The fine hour of the harness costs 0.136 CPU-hours** on one timed-lane core (136 µs per
  step end to end). The operators take 21–25% of W0's step, so N1's gain is bounded at
  1.27–1.34×.
- **M5. On W0 the energy check refuses nothing up to 5 ms,** in either junction booking, and the
  10 ms arms are refused by the junction-gate bound. Below that bound the energy check does not
  hold N2's step on W0.
  - Its largest ratio is at the first step, at most 1.4% of the bound. That step has the
    largest relative residual |R|/F.
  - After the first second it is second order, as FR-18 found on other worlds.
  - The trapezoid booking halves the first-step ratio but is about 1.5 times the default's
    after it.
- **M4. After a channel step or a regulatory write, W0 settles within about a second.**
  - At 10⁻⁴ V/s it takes 1.1 s after the channel step and 0.4 s after the write, and none after
    the dose, whose response is quasi-static.
  - The registered budget rule asks for θ_V ≤ 7.9 × 10⁻⁶ V/s. That lies below every registered
    threshold and below the world's own background, so the rule selects none.
- **An engine gap** for FR-8's fine windows: projective's `settling` readout cannot read a
  composite's channel gates (amendment 2; §7).

## 7. An engine gap found on the way

**An engine gap: projective's settling readout cannot read a composite's channel gates**
(amendment 2). The lead routes it to the composite's owner. Stage 0 fills no engine gap.
- `models/surface_pump_projective.settling` serves @1–@3. It looks for the channel targets with
  `getattr(values, "channel_targets", None)`.
- A composite's values nest the inner values under `ionic`, so on a composite the lookup finds
  nothing. The readout then silently omits the channel gates' distances and reads only the
  junction gates.
- FR-8's fine windows (the note's §4.2) need a settling readout for composites.
- **What M4 does meanwhile.** It evaluates `settling` on the composite's own inner model, effective
  control and view (`model.inner`, `model.inner_state`, `model.control_view`, `model.view`). Those
  are the same inner readouts the composite's own `readouts` computes, so no physics is
  re-derived.
- **The direct composite call** is recorded at every 100th kept row, to show the gap.
- **Checked directly** (`records-c5183aba/m4/gap-check.json`, read-only):
  - the composite's values have the fields `field`, `derivative`, `ionic` and `valid`, so no
    `channel_targets`;
  - at W0's initial row, the channel gates' largest distance is 0.022 and the junction gates'
    1.8 × 10⁻⁴;
  - `settling` on the composite reports 1.8 × 10⁻⁴, 124 times too small; on the inner model at
    the composite's view it reports 0.022.
