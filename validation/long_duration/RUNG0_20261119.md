## 1. The finding to explain

Stage 0's harness H2 (`RESULTS_20261118.md` §4.2 and §9) has a 0.3 s 9× Na-leak cue at
1800–1800.3 s, which switches cell_0 from the H branch to D. Its observed order, at the step
pairs 1–0.5–0.25 ms, varies by phase:
- about 2.3 before the cue;
- 1.25–1.40 in cell_0's switch transient;
- 1.4–1.7 in the error the switch leaves behind.

## 2. The candidate causes, and what the code says

- **(A) The cue's discontinuity:** a 0.3 s step input. If an edge fell between step boundaries,
  the step containing it would see a non-smooth right-hand side.
  - On the registered grids both edges fall on boundaries: stage 0's runs had no off-nominal
    boundary, and every grid here is 2^-k ms on the same 1 ms products.
  - A one-step method with its control held per step and its edges on boundaries keeps its order
    through a derivative jump. So (A) is not expected, and it is tested anyway.
- **(B) The gate updates.** `numerics/exponential_gates.py` (`exponential_mean_rate_linear_target_
  gates@1`) states: "For smooth V the local error is O(h³) (second order), provided the end
  voltage has local error O(h²)." As h/τ grows the update tends to the quasi-steady value with
  its first-order lag. (B) is a defect in that form, or in its coupling to the stage voltages,
  that leaves a first-order term in the transient as dt → 0.
- **(C) The fast nonlinear transient of the switch itself,** unresolved at the stage-0 steps.
  - NaV1.2's fastest rates reach about 6800 s⁻¹ (M2), so dt × rate is about 7, 3.4 and 1.7 at
    1, 0.5 and 0.25 ms.
  - The asymptotic order needs dt × rate ≪ 1, so (C) is pre-asymptotic behaviour that recovers
    second order under refinement.
- **(D) A non-smooth law crossed by the transient.** Read from the code:
  - NaV1.2's rates use `bernoulli`, whose series branch at |x| < 10⁻⁴ is crossed at V = −25,
    −40 and −65 mV. Its truncation is about 10⁻²⁹, so it is smooth to rounding, and the band it
    covers is under 1 µV wide.
  - Kir2.1's two logistic forms are equal analytically.
  - The constant-field flux's branch sits at V ≈ 0, which cell_0 never reaches (it peaks at
    −5.8 mV).
  - So (D) is not expected. Its signature is an order that never settles under refinement.

## 3. The windows and the arms

Each window is a new run that imports R3's saved full state at its start (stage 0's R3: H2 at
0.25 ms from snapshot `24e516c7`, its kept rows) into H2 at dt = 2^-k ms. It runs to the window's
end, and is observed at every 1 ms row. A window that ends before the cue's off-edge drops that
schedule entry, which lies beyond its horizon.

| Window | Start (R3's row) | End | Holds | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WS | 1790.000 s | 1791.000 s | no edge, the slow phase | **Control:** the instrument must give order ≈ 2 here |
| WA | 1799.990 s | 1801.000 s | both edges, the upswing and the downswing | The registered drop, with the edges |
| WC | 1800.010 s | 1800.299 s | the upswing, no edge | **(A)'s clean test:** the upswing without either edge |
| WB | 1800.300 s | 1801.000 s | the downswing, no edge | One-way: if it drops, the edges are not needed |

- **The arms:** k = 0, 1, …, 9 (dt from 1 ms down to 1.95 µs) for every window, with the same runner (windowed, device row checks,
  2048-step windows).
- **Cost:** the feasibility smoke measured the clock's validation at 0.6–0.8 µs per declared
  step from t = 0, so about 24 minutes per window at k = 0–9 (k = 10 was feasible at 600 s but
  is not used).
- **A refusal is a result:** it is kept, and it ends its arm.

## 4. The readings

- **Classes, at each 1 ms row:**
  - the membrane voltages, in V;
  - the concentrations, as |Δc|/c, every species in every cell;
  - the gates by law: NaV m, NaV h, Kir m and Kir h, each over both cells;
  - all gates together.
- **The deciding statistic, one per span and class:** the ratio of sup-norms.
  - D_k = max over the span's rows of |z_k − z_{k+1}|;
  - p_k = log2(D_k / D_{k+1}), for k up to 7.

  It survives sign changes in the error. Everything else is reported, not decided on.
- **Readable pairs.** A pair (k, k+1) is readable if D_{k+1} ≥ 10 F_{k+1}, where F_{k+1} =
  N_{k+2} ε s:
  - N_{k+2} is the finer arm's steps in the window;
  - ε is float64's epsilon;
  - s is 0.1 V, 1 or 1 for voltage, relative concentration and gates.

  That allows a systematic rounding of one ε per step. An unreadable p_k is reported as such.
- **The spans:**
  - WS: (1790.000, 1791.000];
  - WA's pre-edge span: (1799.990, 1800.000];
  - WA's post-edge span: (1800.000, 1800.010], before the upswing;
  - WA's transient: (1800.050, 1800.400];
  - WC: (1800.010, 1800.299];
  - WB: (1800.300, 1801.000].
- **The bands:**
  - second order: p in [1.8, 2.2];
  - first order: p in [0.7, 1.3];
  - an order that settles between the bands, or does not settle, is "not identified".

## 5. The criterion, stated before any registered window runs

**Instrument checks come first.** If one fails, no cause is identified, and the readings are
reported only.
- **I1. The order function** returns 2, 1 and "no order" on the synthetic 16:4:1 and 4:2:1
  sequences and on one whose finer difference sits at its floor.
- **I2. The slow-phase control:** WS gives p_k in [1.8, 2.2] for every class at every readable
  pair.
- **I3. The tie-back to stage 0:** WA at k = 2 equals R3's kept rows on WA's 1 ms rows, bitwise
  expected.
  - If it is not bitwise, the largest difference is reported.
  - The import is faithful if that difference is at most 10⁻³ of WA's transient D_2 for voltage.
    Otherwise the restart is not faithful, and no cause is identified.
- **I4. Reported, not decided on:** WA's k = 0 against k = 2 in its transient, beside stage 0's
  R1-against-R3 figures (2.97 µV and 6.3 × 10⁻⁵ in gate).

**Then the causes.** More than one may be identified. None is a valid result.
- **(C), the pre-asymptotic transient,** is identified if, in WA's transient:
  - for every class, p_k lies in [1.8, 2.2] at the two finest readable pairs;
  - and, in some class, p_k < 1.6 at a readable pair with dt_k ≥ 0.25 ms.
- **(B), a gate-update order defect,** is identified if, in WA's transient, WC or WB, the gate
  class or one gate law has p_k in [0.7, 1.3] at the two finest readable pairs.
- **(A), the discontinuity,** is implicated by any of:
  - **(A1)** an arm with an off-nominal boundary;
  - **(A2)** WA's post-edge span has p_k ≤ 1.6 at a readable pair where its pre-edge span has
    p_k ≥ 1.8;
  - **(A3)** WC, the upswing without an edge, has p_k ≥ 1.8 in every class at every readable
    pair with dt_k ≤ 1 ms, while WA's transient has p_k < 1.6 in some class at one of those
    pairs.
- **(A) is excluded as necessary** if WC or WB, neither holding an edge, has p_k < 1.6 in some
  class at a readable pair with dt_k ≥ 0.25 ms.
- **(D), a non-smooth law,** is identified if, in WA's, WC's or WB's span, some class's p_k
  varies by more than 0.5 over its three finest readable pairs, with the finest of them outside
  both bands.
- **Not identified:** anything else, such as an order that settles between the bands.

**If (C), what is unresolved.** From the finest arm's rows of WA:
- the largest dt × (fastest gate rate, max 1/τ over the gated channels) and dt × `membrane_rate`
  over the transient, for each k, from the engine's own readouts;
- the crossover: the largest dt_k at which p_k first reaches the second-order band.

## 6. The deliverable: a reference whose error is estimated through the switch

- **The Richardson estimate.** For WA (and WC and WB), at the finest readable pair whose p_k lies
  in [1.5, 2.5], the finer arm's error is D_k / (2^p − 1).
- **Which step meets stage 0's budgets.** For every arm, its sup-norm difference from WA's finest
  arm over WA's whole span. The largest dt_k within stage 0's adequacy budgets is reported: 2 µV,
  2 × 10⁻⁵ relative concentration and 10⁻⁵ gate.

## 7. What each outcome implies, stated before

- **(C):**
  - the fine engine is second order through the switch once resolved, and the stage-0 steps
    (≥ 0.25 ms) do not resolve it;
  - every later study's reference through an intervention needs fine windows at or below the
    crossover step, or §6's estimated reference;
  - the exponential form that rung 1's (a) would copy is not implicated.
- **(B):**
  - the exponential gate form has an order defect in transients, so rung 1's (a) must not copy
    it as it is;
  - a corrected gate update comes first.
- **(A):** later studies align events with steps, or refine at edges.
- **(D):** the law named needs a smoothness review before rung 1.
- **Not identified:** rungs 1 and 2 use the most refined reference that §6 can estimate, with
  the order drop as a stated limit.
