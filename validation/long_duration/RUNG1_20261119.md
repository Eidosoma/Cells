## 4. The readings

**The error at a row** is read per class, as at rung 0: voltage per patch, relative concentration per
species and cell, and gate per gate. It is taken against the reference at the same time; re-based
rows are matched by their integer millisecond.

**The budgets.** Both are inherited, and both are read:
- **B_P, the parity caps** (stage 0's §0, projective's `criteria.absolute_caps`):
  - |ΔVm| ≤ 20 µV on every patch;
  - |Δc|/c ≤ 2 × 10⁻⁴ for every species in every cell.

  No gate cap is inherited, so under B_P the gate error is reported as information and judges
  nothing.
- **B_A, stage 0's reference-adequacy budgets** (§5.3; rung 0's `budgets`):
  - |ΔVm| ≤ 2 µV;
  - |Δc|/c ≤ 2 × 10⁻⁵;
  - |Δg| ≤ 10⁻⁵.
- **No other cap is set.** A cap not inherited from stage 0's registration would have to be
  justified without reference to today's measurements, or stay information.

**The phases:**
- **(S),** the rows before the first intervention: on H2, t < 1800 s; on W0, t < 20 s.
- **(I),** every row of the run: on H2 through the cue to 3600 s; on W0 through the dose and the
  write to 60 s.

**The rows read.** Each arm is read at the reference rows that lie on its grid.
- **Common rows, the basis of dt\* and the gain.** For each world and reading, these are the rows on
  the coarsest grid among that reading's arms:
  - on H2, R3's rows on the 100 ms grid for (I), and the 10 s rows for (S);
  - on W0, the 1 s rows.

  Every arm of both IDs is read there, so the two IDs are compared on the same rows. A window rung
  is read at the common rows inside its window.
- **Own rows, reported beside them.** Each arm is also read at every reference row on its grid. That
  uses all the rows a fine arm has, and it favours coarse arms, which see fewer rows (§8).

**Deciding a row.** Let e be the reference's error bound at the row (§3) and b the budget.
- The row passes when |Δ| + e ≤ b.
- It fails when |Δ| − e > b.
- Otherwise it is undetermined.

This carries the reference's own error into the decision and adds no new number.

**Passing.** An arm passes a reading under a budget when both hold:
- it completes, with every row accepted by the engine's checks, X2 included;
- every row of the reading passes, cumulative from t = 0.

If no row fails but some row is undetermined, the arm's reading is undetermined, and a dt* it would
decide is reported as undetermined.

**dt\***, for a world, ID, reading and budget, is the largest rung on the ID's ladder that passes,
provided every finer rung of that ladder passes too. If none passes, dt* is "none on the ladder".

**On H2, the (I) ladder joins the windows and the full runs.** The windows supply the rungs below
1 ms; the full runs supply 1 ms and up, and at 1 ms both are read. (S) on H2 is read on the full runs
alone.
- A window rung is read inside its window only: at the common rows for dt*, and at its 1 ms rows
  beside them. The windows hold the switch and its first second, where stage 0 found every worst
  error; in the slow phases the 1 ms error is below 1 nV.
- That H2's other rows also pass at window rungs is an inference, stated as such.

**The gain,** for a world, reading and budget, is dt*(new) / dt*(today). It is undefined where
either is "none on the ladder".

**Also reported:**
- **The order.** Rung 0's order function (its readable floor at factor 10; its second-order band
  [1.8, 2.2]) is applied on each window's arms and on the halving pairs of W0 and of H2's full runs.
- **What binds,** for each arm that fails: the first refusal (its check, message, time and row), or
  the first budget breach (its class, row and value).
- **X2's record per arm:**
  - its counted steps;
  - q's median and distribution;
  - its held steps;
  - the largest Σ|a|/F against the cap.

  readings, not the instrument.

**I3, X2 under the new ID.** These are X2's qualification rows

  new step, as FR-18's replay harness did under today's scheme.

**I4:** the order function returns 2, 1 and "no order" on the synthetic sequences, as rung 0's I1.

**X2's admission of the control** on each window decides fallback (ii) for that window, not the
instrument.

**The outcomes,** for each world, reading and budget, with the threshold of 10 as proposed:
- **a gain of 10 or more:** converged within budget at dt*, development, with X2 on;
- **a gain above 1 and below 10:** a bounded gain, with what binds named;
- **a gain of 1 or less:** no gain, and the ID stays opt-in;
- **undefined:** reported, naming the side with no passing rung.

In every case the dt* values, the raw gains and what binds are reported, under both budgets.
- **An X2 refusal at a coarse step** is "what binds", not an instrument failure, provided I3 passes

- **FR-8's acceptance reading is (I),** because its case is an intervention during the slow phase.

## 8. Limits, stated now

- **The scope:** two worlds, one cue on H2, and two interventions on W0 over 60 s.
- **The references** are the engine's own fine arms, so the work is development.
- **The rows outside H2's windows** at rungs below 1 ms are inferred (§4).
- **Row density.** A coarse arm sees fewer rows, so its error between them goes unread. dt* and the
  gain are therefore read on common rows, with the own-row readings beside them. Neither reads an
  error that falls between the rows of the coarsest arm.
- **Undetermined rows.** A reference's bound can leave a reading undetermined. This happens where the
  reference's observed order is below 1, or where its error is close to the budget.
- **I3** tests X2's binding and cap under the new ID, not a misbooking planted inside the new step.
- **X2's evidence** ended at 5 ms before rung 1. Rung 1's coarse arms are its first use beyond that.
