## 0. Rules

These are stage 0's §0 and rung 0's rules, carried over.
- **Declared before any comparison runs.** A budget is cumulative from t = 0 and holds at every row
  read, never per step.
- **No engine tolerance or check is loosened, bypassed or replayed around.** A refusal is a result:
  its check, message and time are reported, and it ends that arm.
- **Amendments** are dated additions in §10, written before the run they affect.
- **Every arm selects X2** (D224):
  `execution.energy_check = {"check": "step_doubled_energy@1", "integration_error_cap": 0.002}`.
  - The cap is fixed at today's registered value, stage 0's `energy_rtol_ars222` (the lead's
    ruling).
  - Arms run through the scalar runner's windows (`run_windowed`), which is X2's scope.
- **Provenance.**
  - Every run starts from a clean snapshot of a committed head (`validation/source_snapshot.freeze`),
    and its record names the snapshot's revision.
  - The outputs go under `/mnt/workspaces/Cells/fr8-rung1-20261119/`.
- **Imports under rule (b)** follow rung 0's §9.5 rule. A run that imports a saved state computes
  the source file's sha256 at run time and refuses a mismatch with the digest registered here. It
  records in its own inputs:
  - the digest;
  - the source's producing revision, in full;
  - the source record's identity.
- **CPUs:** the light CPUs 6, 7, 22 and 23 at nice 10, through `scripts/run_detached.sh` with a
  memory cap.
- **Wording.** The references are the engine's own fine arms, so a passing reading is "converged
  within budget, development". It is not a qualification; a judged protocol would be its own
  registration.

## 2. The method under test, and its staging

**N2.** Each membrane's and each junction's slope conductance joins the IMEX operator beside the face
conductances, in (C + a dt L) φ = q, evaluated at the tableau's operator time.
- The species move in flux form with the same conductances, so material and charge stay exact.
- The matrix stays SPD and the same size, with one factorization per step.
- These stay explicit:
  - the pumps' electrogenic currents;
  - the kinds;
  - the operators;
  - face diffusion.

**(a).** The junction gate advances by the exponential update in the form the channel gates use, not
by the tableau's explicit weights.
- The junction's law is unchanged.
- That the channel form suits junction gates is rung 0's inference (§9.4), which rung 1 tests.
- Under the new ID the inherited 0.125 junction-gate bound (5.18 ms on these sheets) is lifted.
  Under today's ID it stays.

**The switch.** The method is one new time-integrator ID under D147, selected by `execution.solver`,
on the ARS(2,2,2) tableau.
- **Its name:** its builder names it in a dated addition to §10 before its first run.
- **Default off:** G1 and crossrev are identical when it is off; that is the ID's landing gate.
- **The guard:** it refuses to run unless X2 is selected (the lead's ruling, part of the ID's
  landing), so no coarse-step run lacks an accuracy guard.
- **X2's scope** admits the new ID in the same landing, with I3 (§5) as its evidence.
- **The composite** reads the same `execution.solver`.

**The build.** The @3 owner builds it after review 4 lands, in two stages: N2 alone first, then (a).
§6 is the one measurement of N2 alone, before (a) is built.

## 4. The readings

**The error at a row** is read per class, as at rung 0: voltage per patch, relative concentration per
species and cell, and gate per gate. It is taken against the reference at the same time; re-based
rows are matched by their integer millisecond.

**The budgets.** Both are inherited, and both are read:
- **B_P, the parity caps** (stage 0's §0, projective's `criteria.absolute_caps`):
  - |ΔVm| ≤ 20 µV on every patch;
  - |Δc|/c ≤ 2 × 10⁻⁴ for every species in every cell.

  No gate cap is inherited, so under B_P the gate error is reported as information and judges
  nothing.
- **B_A, stage 0's reference-adequacy budgets** (§5.3; rung 0's `budgets`):
  - |ΔVm| ≤ 2 µV;
  - |Δc|/c ≤ 2 × 10⁻⁵;
  - |Δg| ≤ 10⁻⁵.
- **No other cap is set.** A cap not inherited from stage 0's registration would have to be
  justified without reference to today's measurements, or stay information.

**The phases:**
- **(S),** the rows before the first intervention: on H2, t < 1800 s; on W0, t < 20 s.
- **(I),** every row of the run: on H2 through the cue to 3600 s; on W0 through the dose and the
  write to 60 s.

**Passing.** An arm passes a reading under a budget when both hold:
- it completes, with every row accepted by the engine's checks, X2 included;
- its error is within the budget at every row of the reading, cumulative from t = 0.

**dt\***, for a world, ID, reading and budget, is the largest rung on the ID's ladder that passes,
provided every finer rung of that ladder passes too. If none passes, dt* is "none on the ladder".

**On H2, the ladder joins the windows and the full runs.** The windows supply the rungs below 1 ms;
the full runs supply 1 ms and up, and at 1 ms both are read.
- A window rung is read on the window's rows only. Those hold the switch and its first second, where
  stage 0 found every worst error; in the slow phases the 1 ms error is below 1 nV.
- That H2's other rows also pass at window rungs is an inference, stated as such.

**The gain,** for a world, reading and budget, is dt*(new) / dt*(today). It is undefined where
either is "none on the ladder".

**Also reported:**
- **The order.** Rung 0's order function (its readable floor at factor 10; its second-order band
  [1.8, 2.2]) is applied on each window's arms and on the halving pairs of W0 and of H2's full runs.
- **What binds,** for each arm that fails: the first refusal (its check, message, time and row), or
  the first budget breach (its class, row and value).
- **X2's record per arm:**
  - its counted steps;
  - q's median and distribution;
  - its held steps;
  - the largest Σ|a|/F against the cap.

## 5. The criterion, stated before any run

**Instrument checks.** If I1–I4 do not all pass, the verdict is "instrument not validated", and no
dt* or gain is read.

**I1, consistency:**
- **I1a, the re-basing and X2.** On each window, the control at k = 9 matches rung 0's arm at k = 9
  within rung 0's readable floor. On WA, the control at k = 2 matches R3's kept rows within that
  floor; this was bitwise at rung 0, as its I3.
- **I1b, the new ID.** The new ID's finest arm matches today's finest arm within the readable floor
  plus both arms' estimated errors: on H2's windows at k = 9, against the control; on W0, at
  0.125 ms.

**I2, the reference's adequacy:**
- At every row read, the reference's estimated error is at most a tenth of the budget the row is
  read under.
- On W0, the observed order from the 0.5, 0.25 and 0.125 ms arms lies in the second-order band.
- A row that fails I2 under a budget leaves that budget's reading undetermined at that row, and a dt*
  it would decide is reported as undetermined.

**I3, X2 under the new ID.** These are X2's qualification rows
(`validation/energy_step_doubling/qualify_x2.py`), run with `execution.solver` set to the new ID,
under X2's own pass conditions (its `judge`). The same rows serve X2's admission of the ID in its
landing.
- **The plant,** FR-3's registered plant (`run_plant`): the control resume is admitted, and the
  planted resume is refused with "does not hold its state's residual".
- **The cap control** (`run_cap`): refused with "exceeds the registration's cap at accepted step 1".
- **A limit, stated now:** these test X2's binding and its cap. Neither plants a misbooking inside the
  new step, as FR-18's replay harness did under today's scheme.
- If a row's world cannot run the new ID, I3 is untested, and that is an instrument failure.

**I4:** the order function returns 2, 1 and "no order" on the synthetic sequences, as rung 0's I1.

**X2's admission of the control** on each window decides fallback (ii) for that window, not the
instrument.

**The outcomes,** for each world, reading and budget, with the threshold of 10 as proposed:
- **a gain of 10 or more:** converged within budget at dt*, development, with X2 on;
- **a gain above 1 and below 10:** a bounded gain, with what binds named;
- **a gain of 1 or less:** no gain, and the ID stays opt-in;
- **undefined:** reported, naming the side with no passing rung.

In every case the dt* values, the raw gains and what binds are reported, under both budgets.
- **An X2 refusal at a coarse step** is "what binds", not an instrument failure, provided I3 passes
  (the lead's ruling).
- **FR-8's acceptance reading is (I),** because its case is an intervention during the slow phase.

**The prediction, stated before any run:**
- **H2 through the cue:** (I) is limited by NaV's activation rate (rung 0's outcome (C)), which
  neither N2 nor (a) touches. So H2's gain in (I) is predicted to be about 1 under both budgets. If
  so, FR-8's acceptance case needs rung 2's fine windows.
- **The slow phase:** any gain is predicted in (S): on H2 past today's membrane charge bound
  (71.4 ms), and on W0 past today's junction-gate bound (5.18 ms).
- **W0 through its interventions:** (I) is not predicted. The write's response settles in about
  0.4 s (stage 0's M4); whether a coarse step through it stays within budget is the reading's
  question.

## 7. What each outcome implies (stated before)

- **A gain of about 1 in (I) on both worlds (predicted).** The new ID gives nothing through an
  intervention. FR-8's acceptance case then needs rung 2's fine windows around each intervention,
  with the new ID in the slow phases between them if (S) gains.
- **A gain of 10 or more in (S) on W0.** N2 with (a) has converged within budget on a gated sheet's
  slow phase at dt*, development. Rung 2 builds its fine windows on it.
- **A bounded gain, or none, in (S).** What binds sets rung 2's design: X2's cap, a budget, a step
  check, the operators' coupling condition at 30 s, or the pumps. With no gain, rung 2's slow
  integrator (c) is the lever.
- **"Instrument not validated."** Rung 1 stops, as rung 0 did, and the failing check goes to the
  lead.
