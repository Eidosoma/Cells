# FR-49's registration, reading 3 (28 November 2026): C1-1's physical rows

This is a dated reading of `validation/fr49/REGISTRATION.md` §5's C1-1, ruled by the lead before the Parts B and C
judge's tag and before any registered C1 run (D308, D424).

**The criterion:** C1-1 holds when O's and C's physical rows and label shares are bit for bit the twin's on every row.

**The reading, the stricter one.** "O's and C's physical rows" covers every physical state field at O and at C, not
only the species rows and the shares:
- every state field on the cell axis, taken at O;
- every state field on the node axis, taken at C;
- the label fields (`judge_l.LABELS_ONLY`) aside, since the shares are compared on their own.

So C1-1 compares the species amounts, and the books of every law that keeps state at O or C: the reservoir, the
electrode (the node's charge transferred among them), turnover, polymerisation and the wash. C1's state holds no voltage
field.

**How it is judged.**
- The driver saves each field on every row in the npz, as `O.<field>` and `C.<field>` (`driver_bc.physical_at`).
- The judge derives the set it expects from the engine's own state axes for C1's world (`judge_bc.c1_physical_names`).
- It refuses a record that lacks one of them, and compares each bit for bit, beside the species rows and the shares.

**C1's world at 3d427700** gives 28 such arrays.
- At O, 10: `amount_increment`, `poly_consumed_fmol`, `poly_dissipated_energy_pJ`, `poly_energy_magnitude_pJ`,
  `poly_produced_fmol`, `poly_standard_energy_pJ`, `turnover_cleared_fmol`, `turnover_energy_magnitude_pJ`,
  `turnover_produced_fmol` and `turnover_work_pJ`.
- At C, 18: `electrode_charge_transferred_C`, `electrode_work_pJ`, `extracellular_amount_increment`,
  `reservoir_supplied`, `reservoir_work_pJ`, `turnover_outside_cleared_fmol`, `turnover_outside_energy_magnitude_pJ`,
  `turnover_outside_work_pJ`, `wash_applied`, `wash_buffer_free_energy_pJ`, `wash_collected_fmol`,
  `wash_energy_magnitude_pJ`, `wash_last_mixing_heat_pJ`, `wash_last_sample_fmol`, `wash_last_supply_fmol`,
  `wash_mixing_heat_pJ`, `wash_sample_free_energy_pJ` and `wash_supplied_fmol`.

**A development check.** The builder ran a shortened pair, C1 and its twin to 1260 s at dt 2 s, through the driver. All 28
arrays are bit for bit the twin's, and 13 of them are nonzero. This is development evidence, not a result.

**Tested** in `tests/unit/test_fr49_judge_bc.py`:
- a record whose node electrode charge differs from the twin's fails C1-1;
- a record lacking one of the arrays is refused.
