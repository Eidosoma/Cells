# FR-49's registration, reading 3 (28 November 2026): C1-1's physical rows, and C1-2's well checks

These are dated readings of `validation/fr49/REGISTRATION.md` §5's C1-1 and C1-2, ruled by the lead before the Parts B
and C judge's tag and before any registered C1 run (D308, D424).

## C1-1: every non-label field whose entity is O or C

**The criterion:** C1-1 holds when O's and C's physical rows and label shares are bit for bit the twin's on every row.

**The reading, the lead's ruling** (after the reviewer's third pass). "O's and C's physical rows" covers every
non-label state field whose entity is O or C, and the books of the patches that bound them:
- **O's state on any axis:** its cell-axis fields, and every expression field whose entity is O (its chains, its
  complexes, its machine pool, its holds and its degradations);
- **C's node fields;**
- **every patch bounding O or C, except one with W on a side,** with its `membrane_*` and `passive_*` books and any face
  on it. In C1's world that is the O–bath wall (`patch_0`) and the O–C patch (`patch_export_cell_0_space_0`).

**Excluded by the ruling:** W's node, and the patch, faces and routes between W and O. The wash may legitimately touch
those.

**How it is judged.**
- `validation/fr49/worlds_bc.py` holds the rule as one table (`C1_CLASS`, `C1_SUB_AXES`, `C1_EMPTY_AXES`,
  `c1_entity_slots`, `c1_part`), so the driver and the judge class each axis the same way:
  - an entity axis is cut to O's slots, C's slot, or the slots of the patches that bound them (W's aside);
  - a sub-axis (species, gate, a chain's stage, a hold's class or fate) is kept whole;
  - an axis with no slot in C1's world must stay empty;
  - an axis the rule does not class is refused.
- The driver saves each compared part on every row, as `O.<field>`, `C.<field>` or `P.<field>`
  (`driver_bc.physical_at`).
- The judge derives the names it expects from the engine's own state axes for C1's world (`judge_bc.c1_physical_names`).
  It refuses a record that lacks one, and compares each bit for bit, beside the species rows and the shares.

**The classification of C1's state.** Label fields are compared as shares, and are not physical rows: `label_share`,
`label_outside_share`, `label_closure_relative`, `label_pool_share`, `label_removed_fmol`, `label_pool_removed_fmol`
and `label_outside_removed_fmol`. Every other field is classed below.

So C1-1 compares 49 arrays besides the species rows and the shares: 21 of O's, 18 of C's, and 10 of the patches that
bound them. Each of the 8 patch fields holds two slots, the O–bath wall's and the O–C patch's.

**What is excluded, and why:**
- **W's slots:** the well's node (`well_w`) on the node fields, and the W–O patch (`patch_cell_0_well_w`) on the patch
  fields. This is the ruling's exclusion: the wash may touch them.
- **The bath's node** (`bath_0`, a held reservoir) on the node fields. Its entity is the bath, not O or C. The wall
  between O and the bath is a patch bounding O, so it is compared.
- **The face slot:** C1 declares no face, so the slot belongs to no entity.
- **Axes with no slot:** there is nothing to compare, and the rule refuses the judgement if one gains a slot.

C1's state holds no voltage field. Its only charge field is the nodes' `electrode_charge_transferred_C`, which is
compared at C.

The widened set's pair runs after 03:15 UTC, and its result is recorded below. This is development evidence, not a

## C1-2: the well's cross-checks, by exact name

C1-2's inconclusive rule reads the well's own cross-checks. A cross-check is the well's only if its name directly under
`method` is exactly one of `WELL_METHODS`, the lead's accepted list:
- `well`, the lead's key;
- `well_second_route`, the name of the references' invented format sample's only well check
  (`method.well_second_route.within`). With `well` alone, the reader refuses that sample.

A key not on the list is refused, never matched by a bare prefix: `method.wellX`, `method.wells` and
`method.well_second_routeX` are not the well's. The builder does not open the real C1.json, so its key is unverified,
and a C1 file with no check on the list is refused, not read as all within.

**Tested** in `tests/unit/test_fr49_judge_bc.py`:
- a record whose node electrode charge differs from the twin's fails C1-1;
- a record lacking one of the arrays is refused;
- the entity rule cuts a patch field to the O–C patch, and refuses an axis it does not class, a field with no entity
  axis, and an empty axis that holds a slot;
- the well's names are matched exactly.
