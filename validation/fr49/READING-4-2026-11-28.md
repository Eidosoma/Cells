## Plant (e)'s layout check

**The text** (§A2.6): "Plant (e) checks, before it patches, that the engine's binding layout is the one it was written
against, as C1's plant does."

**The reading.** The check compares two things with what the plant was written against:
- the binding layout's field names, in their order;
- two values at bind: one population binding G, and one pool.

A field gained, lost or reordered raises before the patch, and `bind` is left untouched. "As C1's plant does" is read as
the same guard, checked before the patch against what the plant was written for. It is not read as the same method:
this check does not compare statements, as C1's plant does.

**The evidence.** A reviewer who is not the builder showed the check firing on each of those changes, and on a drifted
engine copy run through the driver, where the run ended with outcome `error` and no step. With the identical list it
passes. The review is in `/mnt/workspaces/Cells/fr49-c4-plant-review-20261128/`.

## A plant record that does not complete

A plant run forms no catch when its record's outcome is anything but `completed`. That covers a refusal by a named
check, and a stop by the plant's own guard with outcome `error`. Its part fails (§A2.6; READING-2's reading 2).
`judge_c4` reads it so: such a plant is scored `caught: false`.
