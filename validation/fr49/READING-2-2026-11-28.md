# FR-49's registration, reading 2 (28 November 2026): the Parts B and C judge's readings

This is a dated record of the lead's rulings on the seven readings that `validation/fr49/judge_bc.py` embodies, and of
four changes to the judge that the rulings add. A reviewer who is not the builder checked the plants and these readings
first, at 287a5ffa. It is recorded before the judge's tag and before any registered World B, C1 or C2 run (D308, D424).
Each of the four changes closes a way in which a pass could be made easier.

## The seven readings

1. **Confirmed.** C1-2's "just after the wash" is the 902 s row, the first saved row after the wash at 900 s at dt 2 s.
   "Just before" is the 900 s row. The judge reads both.
2. **Confirmed.** A plant's catch is a registered criterion's failure. A plant run refused by a named check is reported,
   and is not the catch, so its part fails.
3. **Enforced, as change 1.** "Every row" is every saved row, so a record's rows must be the dt grid.
4. **Confirmed, as a reading.** World B's twin is one run at 0.1 s, the literal reading of "a twin" (D424).
   - B5 compares the twin with the honest run at 0.1 s.
   - B1 and B2 are judged on the two honest runs and on the twin.
5. **Replaced by change 2.** A completed run's host checks bound the label owner's closure diagnostic only as finite and
   non-negative, so completion alone does not bound it.
6. **Confirmed.**
   - B2's L_T is the declared n_L(0) + B(0), and C2-1's L_T,j the declared totals.
   - B4's and C2-3's equilibria, and B3's and C2-2's references, are the references' values.
   - Plant (a)'s B is formed in the judge from the declared values, in 50-digit decimal arithmetic.
7. **Confirmed.** C2's runs are judged at both steps for C2-1 and C2-3, and C1's at its one step.

## The four changes to the judge

1. **Reading 3, enforced.** A completed record's rows must be exactly the dt grid from 0 to the horizon:
   - horizon/dt + 1 rows;
   - the k-th row at k·dt, the binary64 product, as the runner's grid makes it;
   - the last row at the horizon;
   - every quantity and every array with one entry per row.

   Any other record is refused with a ReaderError (`grid_refusal`, `read_record`). A record on the observation grid
   (5 s, 60 s or 20 s) is therefore refused.
2. **C1-3's closure.** C1-3 now also requires `label_closure_relative` ≤ 64 ε on every row of C1 and of its twin, where ε
   is binary64's machine epsilon (`CLOSURE`).
   - **The bound** is taken from the existing tests of the label owner's own runs, each of which asserts ≤ 64 ε:
     - `tests/unit/test_neutral_labels_kinds.py:139` (`test_kinds_carry_the_labels_and_change_nothing_physical`);
     - `tests/unit/test_neutral_labels_transport.py:120` (`test_routes_carry_the_shares_with_the_exact_rows`);
     - `tests/unit/test_neutral_arrival_labels.py:687` (`test_stage_three_reports_follow_the_material`);
     - `tests/unit/test_neutral_arrival_labels.py:816` (`test_labels_leave_every_record_of_the_unlabelled_run`).

     The line numbers are those at 8fdeeed3.
   - **The development value.** The lead and the reviewer have seen a development value of about 1.4e-16. The builder's
     shortened C1 runs (1260 s at dt 2 s) give 1.42e-16.
   - **The record.** The driver now saves the diagnostic on every row, as the npz array `label_closure_relative`. A
     record without it is refused.
3. **B5.**
   - **Scored for plant (d) only.** Plant (d)'s mechanism is the wash, so B5 counts as its catch. For plants (b) and (c),
     B5 is reported but not scored: their differences from the honest twin are their physics, and their registered
     catches are B2 and B4 (`B5_SCORED`).
   - **Gated on the wash's counts.** These are read from the cumulative `wash_applied` rows:
     - the well's event applied exactly once in each honest run (0.1 s and 0.05 s);
     - none in the twin;
     - none at lys in any of them.

     Otherwise B5 fails, even where the arrays match the twin's bit for bit. A plant run's B5 takes the same gate: once
     at the well, none at lys.
4. **C1's cross-check filter.** C1-2's inconclusive rule reads the well's own cross-checks (`method.well…`). A C1 file
   with none is refused with a ReaderError, both by the reader and by `judge_c1`. It is not read as "all within".
