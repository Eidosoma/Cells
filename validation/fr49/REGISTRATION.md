# FR-49: the registered protocol `fr49-compartment-compositions-v1` and its pass criteria

This note fixes the pass criteria for FR-49, compartment composition qualifications. It was written
on 23 November 2026, before any code-blind reference exists and before any engine code. The design
is `/mnt/workspaces/Cells/fr49-51-design-20261123/DESIGN.md` §1, with rulings 1–9 as D327 approved
them. The lead tags this note and the references' spec (`references/spec/`) before the engine
builds (D327).

**The engine.**
- `resolved_pump_composite@1` over `resolved_pump_tissue@3`;
- `cell_owned_compartments@1`, with its admitted list's four new entries:
  - `templated_polymerisation@1`;
  - `neutral_arrival_labels@1`;

  - `scheduled_compartment_wash@1`;
- in the worlds, `scheduled_species_turnover@1`, `cells.passive_routes@1`
  (`passive_neutral_permeation@1`) and, for C2, FR-45's shared site sets in `site_occupancy@1`.

**When each part is judged** (D327's order: one owner per file at a time):

| Part | What it judges | Judged at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H | The host rows of each piece | Each piece's own landing |
| L | Labels and expression beside a compartment (World L) | The landing of the first pieces, expression and labels |

lander lists what newly parses at its merged head.

**Bounds fixed before any reference value was seen.** Each bound below was fixed before this writer
saw any value from the registered references. What informed them is listed in §6.

**The inconclusive rule.** A criterion is inconclusive, not a pass, where the reference reports a
cross-check difference above its declared accuracy.

## 1. The errors

For a quantity q at the engine's step h, compared at the sample times t_i:

    e_q(h) = max_i |q_engine,h(t_i) − q_ref(t_i)| / max_i |q_ref(t_i)|.

## 3. Part L: World L (labels and expression beside a compartment)

### 3.1 The world

**The engine world** is the expression world, with:
- `validation.gene_regulation.worlds.with_export(r1(0.75))`, its export and outside degradation
  removed, so expression's routes are in cell_0 only;
- `space_0` declared as cell_0's compartment, with its volume set to V_C;
- a neutral species S added, with:
  - a `passive_neutral_permeation@1` route on the bounding patch (area 1e-10 m²), with
    permeability a/(1e15 × 1e-10 m²) = 2e-9 m/s;
  - turnover's production in cell_0 at P and clearance at `space_0` at k_d;
- S labelled, with `label_arrivals` giving x from t₁ and the base from t₂;
- the bounding patch's own permeabilities zero, and the compartment's charge anchor zero: the
  silent version, so S1–S3 test silence and not a leak.

Every value of S's system is `cases.json`'s world L. The builder records the mapping, and changes
no value.

**The runs:**
- dt = 2 s and 1 s, to the horizon of 3600 s, observed every 60 s;
- for each step, its unlabelled twin;
- one restart, at dt = 2 s.

### 3.2 The criteria

1. **L1, the host checks.** Every host check passes on every row of every run.
2. **L2, the physics is untouched.** Every physical field, observation and diagnostic of the
   labelled run is bit for bit the unlabelled twin's, at both steps (`same_physics`).
3. **L3, nothing labelled before the pulse.** Before t₁, x_O and x_C are exactly 0 on every row.
4. **L4, the order.** For q in {n_O, n_C, x_O, x_C}:
   - 3.6 ≤ e_q(2 s)/e_q(1 s) ≤ 4.4;
   - where e_q(2 s) < 1e-8, the ratio is not judged, and q passes if e_q(1 s) < 1e-8.
5. **L5, the size.** e_q(1 s) ≤ 1e-3 for each q of L4.
6. **L6, expression's silence at C.** Every species row of expression's at `space_0` is bitwise its
   declared amount on every row, and S1–S3 hold at `space_0` (`compartment_fixtures.silence`).
7. **L7, restart.** A checkpoint at t = 900 s, inside the pulse, resumed to the horizon, is bit for
   bit the uninterrupted run.

**Plants, each of which must fail L3, L4 or L7:**
- (a) the label owner skips the compartment's column;
- (b) the pulse's label given to transfers: arrivals by the route join the window's label, not their
  source's shares;
- (c) the kind's one-way amounts halved in both directions (FR-42's own plant);
- (d) a checkpoint without `label_outside_share`.

## 4. Part B: World B (lumen sites and a wash elsewhere)

### 4.1 The world

**The engine world** is the review's well beside a specimen cell that owns a compartment `lys`
(the design note's `probe/worlds.py`, `world_b`), with:
- `lys`'s volume set to V_C;
- an outward site population on `lys`'s bounding patch, with `cases.json`'s world B values;
- the ligand at `lys` at n_L(0), and at its declared trace (at most 1e-6 K_d) everywhere else;
- the well washed at t = 300 s with f = 0.5, the buffer at the ligand's trace.

**The runs:**
- dt = 0.1 s and 0.05 s, to 600 s, observed every 5 s;
- a twin with the wash declared and no event;
- the node-owned variant (ruling 2).

### 4.2 The criteria

1. **B1, the host checks.** Every host check passes on every row.
2. **B2, conservation.** |n_L + B − L_T| ≤ 1e-10 L_T on every row, at `lys`.
3. **B3, the transient.**
   - 7.2 ≤ e_B(0.1 s)/e_B(0.05 s) ≤ 8.8. The site operator's half-steps are SSPRK(3,3), and
     binding is the ligand's only mover.
   - Where e_B(0.1 s) < 1e-12, the ratio is not judged, and B passes if e_B(0.05 s) < 1e-12.
4. **B4, the equilibrium.** At every sample with t ≥ 550 s, about 35 relaxation times,
   |B − B*| ≤ 1e-10 B*, at both steps.
5. **B5, the wash elsewhere.** Every row of `lys`'s species and the population's R and B are bit for
   bit the twin's, on every row, before and after the wash.
6. **B6, the node-owned variant.** Its layout arrays are bitwise the outward population's, so its
   run is World B's. A host row checks this; no compile is needed.

**Plants, each of which must fail B2, B4 or B5:**
- (a) no depletion: B from the free-ligand Langmuir with c₀ = L_T/V_C, about 2.9% off B*;
- (b) the population reads the owner's pool (inward facing);
- (c) K_d × 2;
- (d) the wash applied to every finite node.

## 5. Part C: the combination checks (D327)

### C1. A compartment with labels and a wash at a separate node

**The world:** World L, with a well W joined to cell_0 by a patch that carries no S route.
- W's S is declared with initial shares: x 0.6, base 0.4.
- W is washed at 900 s, inside the labelled pulse, with f = 0.5 and the buffer's S at 1e-9 mM.

**The runs:** dt = 2 s, and its twin with the wash declared and no event.

**The criteria:**
1. **C1-1:** O's and C's physical rows and shares are bit for bit the twin's on every row.
2. **C1-2:** W's x and base amounts just after the wash are LAWS.md §4's, within 1e-13 relative.
   The buffer carries the base label although a window is open (D327, FR-44).
3. **C1-3:** every host check passes, the label owner's closure included.

**Plant:** the buffer given the window's label x. It must fail C1-2.

### C2. Competing ligands sharing sites at a compartment

**The world:** World B's compartment, with two ligands on one shared site set (FR-45's), at
`cases.json`'s C2 values, and no wash.

**The runs:** dt = 0.1 s and 0.05 s, to 2000 s.

**The criteria:**
1. **C2-1:** each ligand is conserved, |n_j + B_j − L_T,j| ≤ 1e-10 L_T,j on every row.
2. **C2-2:** for q in {B₁, B₂}, 7.2 ≤ e_q(0.1 s)/e_q(0.05 s) ≤ 8.8, with B3's floor rule.
3. **C2-3:** at every sample with t ≥ 1800 s, about 36 relaxation times of the slower mode,
   |B_j − B_j*| ≤ 1e-10 B_j*.

**Plant:** L₂'s k_on doubled. It must fail C2-3.

### C4. The four-way form

The inherited composition is C1's world: `cell_owned_compartments@1`, `neutral_arrival_labels@1`, and
`scheduled_compartment_wash@1` at an external node. Its four-way form adds unlabelled
`site_occupancy@1`. That form is registered by an amendment of this note once FR-44's Admission by
species lands, and it is refused by name until then.

## 6. Evidence that informed the bounds

The evidence is in `/mnt/workspaces/Cells/fr49-registration-20261123/probe/`. These are
development aids on the declared values, not the code-blind reference, and not engine runs.

**`scales.py`, World L:**
- the eigen-rates are 6.4e-5 and 3.1e-3 /s, so dt λ_max = 0.0063 at 2 s;
- C's labelled share peaks near 0.23 after the pulse;
- Strang's second-order error is then far above L4's 1e-8 floor.

**`scales.py`, World B:**
- 97% of the ligand is bound at equilibrium, with b < 0, the second branch of the root;
- τ = 15.8 s, and dt L = 0.2 at 0.1 s;
- plant (a) misses B* by 2.9%.

**`c2_scales.py`:** C2's slowest relaxation time is 49.7 s, and its remainder at 2000 s is 1.7e-16. (The remainder figure is withdrawn by amendment 1 below; the relaxation time stands.)

**The other bounds:**
- The order windows follow FR-43 part 2's [3.6, 4.4] for second order. For third order the window
  is [7.2, 8.8]; FR-3's Q1 dt study measured 8.39 and 8.19.
- The conservation bound covers the site operator's per-step rounding allowance (2.5 ε per step)
  accumulated in the worst case: 6.7e-12 over World B's 12,000 steps at 0.05 s, and 2.2e-11 over
  C2's 40,000. That is 15 and 4.5 times headroom. A misbooking plant misses by order 1.

## Amendment 1 (24 November 2026): §6's C2 remainder, a development figure withdrawn

**The point.** The code-blind references could not reproduce one figure of §6: C2's remainder at
2000 s. That figure is a development estimate, and it is withdrawn here. The reference reads nothing
differently.

**Why the figure was wrong.** It came from `c2_scales.py`, a development aid in binary64: SciPy's
Radau at rtol 1e-12 and atol 1e-24, against a root from `fsolve` at xtol 1e-15. Its value is that
aid's own floor, the rounding of the integrated state and of the root at binary64's resolution of
B*, not the remainder of the relaxation.
- **The remainder itself** lies far below binary64's resolution of B*, by C2's slowest relaxation time
  (which stands) over 2000 s.
- `probe/c2_remainder.py` shows this. It propagates the departure from equilibrium linearly from 500 s,
  so that no cancellation limits it. Its record is beside the other probes.

**What changes:** only §6's evidence sentence. No criterion, bound or declared value changes.
- C2-3's bound, 1e-10 relative at t ≥ 1800 s, was fixed with this headroom in view.
- The references' own figure stands as they report it.
- This amendment quotes none of the references' values.
