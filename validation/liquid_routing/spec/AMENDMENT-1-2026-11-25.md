# Amendment 1, 25 November 2026: the references agent's six readings, and the layout they settle

**Written by the registration's author at the lead's request, after the criteria tag**
`liquid-routing-criteria-2026-11-25` (e2b19380) and before the references' tag. It answers six points the
code-blind references agent found, as the lead ruled them, and the minor readings it adopted.

- **Bands.** It changes no band, class, window, count, tolerance, plant or value. Each ruling below is a
  clarification of what the tagged files already compare.
- **Value-silent.** It states no reference, engine or planted value.
- **Precedence.** Where it and the files it amends differ (README, SPEC, LAWS, READOUTS, CRITERIA, TABLE,
  `inputs.json`), it governs. Read it with them. A later amendment governs over this one.
- **Read with** the tagged SPEC §4, §7, §8 and §9; READOUTS §1 and §3; CRITERIA §3.2, §4 and §6.2; TABLE §2.

## 1. The default heat's law, its third clause (SPEC §9, check 10)

Check 10's third clause (the ratio of H_def − D(m_R) to −C₂ z² D(m_R), within 8z of 1) holds at **every judged
default start and step**: `eq-mid`, `eq-hi`, `r4-mid` and `r4-hi` at each of their steps. It is asked of those
only. The recorded starts (`eq-tr`, `eq-lo` and `r4-lo` at kh = 1/64) and the `eq-tr` stop (kh = 0.05) lie
outside the expansion's domain, since their moved amount is not small against the lower well's content. Their
values of all three clauses go to a list **`reported`** beside `checks`, in the same form, and are read by no
check. The `eq-mid` stop (kh = 0.1) is asked for by neither list.

**Can a verdict depend on it?** No: it is a check on the references before their tag, not a criterion. C0 reads
`checks` (every entry passes) and never `reported`.

## 2. Plants with no margin (CRITERIA §6.2, SPEC §7)

A class-E plant has no margin. Its `plants` entry holds `"expected_tolerance": "0"`, `"margin": null` and
`"no_margin": true`, and its `distance` must be non-zero (a zero distance is a fault). Every other plant with a
margin holds `"no_margin": false`. C0's "every plant margin at least 10" reads the plants that have one. P8's
H_missing is listed separately, in **`plants_reported`** (one entry per engine plant, no margin), and P18 has no
references' size.

**Can a verdict depend on it?** Yes: C0 refuses a file whose plant with a margin falls below 10, and a class-E
plant with a zero distance.

## 3. The sum of `F7`'s ligand L (CRITERIA §3.2)

For L in `F7` the sum includes the bound amount: Σ_i n_L,i − supplied_L + bound_L_S equals L's start, over A, B,
K and S, formed by the judge from the table's own rows. The sites bind L at the node S, so the free amount alone
does not conserve. The references publish n_L_A, n_L_B, n_L_K,
n_T_S and n_U_S (class R) and no value for `n_L_S`, `free_sites_S` and `bound_L_S`, which the E twin alone
compares (LAWS has no binding law). The R bound is m_n's, as for the other sums.

**Can a verdict depend on it?** Yes: without the bound amount the sum would fail on an honest run; with it, the
sum is what the tagged text intended. No band changes.

## 4. The keys of `planted_series` (SPEC §7)

A planted series keyed `<world>|<step>` (the step's `repr`) where it depends on the step, and `<world>` where it
does not. The step-dependent ones are P7, P11, P14, P15 and P19; the others of LAWS §11's table that need a
series are P2, P3, P4, P5 and P9. P1, P10, P16, P17 and P18 need none: they are formed from the honest rows or
the references.

**Can a verdict depend on it?** No: the judge reads the same planted numbers, by name.

## 5. Arm 6's H_tot, `H5`'s holder heats, and the class of an entry

- **`H_tot` in arm 6** is LAWS §8's total, Σ H_holder + H_face. In `J0`, where every router heat is zero, it is
  H_face alone, and its class-S entry is met by `J0`'s E twin.
- **`H5`'s H_A, H_B and H_K are class U:** each, like their sum, is at most CRITERIA §3.6's bound. READOUTS §1's
  "arm 5: S" for `H_<i>` is for `H1` to `H4` (§3's arm 5 already says so). No value of the bound changes.
- **The classes a reference entry may carry** are E, R, B, S, L and U (SPEC §4 and CRITERIA C0). C0 refuses any
  other.

**Can a verdict depend on it?** Yes, by reading: `H5`'s holders face the U bound, and an entry with a class
outside the six is a fault. The U bound and H_tot's S comparison are unchanged.

## 6. Invented names (SPEC §8, TABLE §2)

In `reference_invented.json` and `inputs_invented.json` every world and run name takes the prefix `inv-`, and
`arm` is 0 in `reference_invented.json`. The builder's rehearsal table carries those same prefixed names (it runs
`inputs_invented.json`'s runs). The judge strips the prefix before it uses its twin and plant tables, and a
rehearsal file or table without the prefix is a fault of the rehearsal (CRITERIA §10).

**Can a verdict depend on it?** No: the rehearsal is evidence for the harness, not the request.

## 7. Minor readings, adopted

- **Constants.** N_A and k_B are read as the nearest doubles of their decimal literals (README, SPEC §0).
- **`levels_exact` and `levels_scheme`** are amounts, `[n_T_1, n_T_2]`, in fmol.
- **`cases`** carry H_def and B_def at every step.
- **Arm 4's `events`** hold `amount` beside `volume`, `loss`, `resident` and `arriving`.
- **Class R, value 0:** a collector's rows after t = 0 and before its first portion, and `label_T_plain_A` at
  t = 0. SPEC §4's E entries at t = 0 stand.
- **`plants_reported`** holds P8's H_missing (item 2).

**Can a verdict depend on these?** No. Each restates what the tagged files already compare or name.
