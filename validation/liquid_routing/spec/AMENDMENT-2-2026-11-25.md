## 1. The sums in arm 2 (CRITERIA §3.2)

**The tagged text was wrong.** "Σ_i n_x,i − supplied_x equals the start" leaves out the sample, which has left the
wells, and arm 2 has no inlet and so no `supplied_T`.

**The ruling.** In arm 2 the sum at a row is n_T_1 + n_T_2 + Σ (sample − supplied) = the start, over the exchanges
whose time is before the row's, with the run's own event numbers. A row at an exchange's time is the state before
it (SPEC §1), so that exchange is not in its sum. In `N-none` the sum is n_T_1 + n_T_2. The tolerance is CRITERIA
§1's arm 2 row with its event term, unchanged.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes.** As tagged, every honest run with an exchange would fail after it.

## 2. The scale of a portion's and an exchange's own amount (READOUTS §2)

**The tagged text was wrong, read literally.** N_x is "start plus supplied to that time", and a row at an event's
time is the state before it. So the first portion into an empty collector had a scale of zero, and a tolerance of
zero.

**The ruling.** The amount a portion or an exchange itself supplies counts in N_x for that event's `amount`,
`loss` and `heat` (for `loss` and `heat`, through the sum in s_F). Every other entry's scale is as tagged.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes.** It gives those entries the registered e_round in place of zero. The first
portion's `loss` stays class E, +0.0, and is not touched.
