(`liquid-routing-amendment-4-2026-11-25`, 49691515), **from the lead's rulings on the spec audit,** before the judge

- **Bands.** It moves no constant, window, scale or existing count. **It does change classes and what is judged:** V1

## V. Text under which an honest engine could not pass, or a wrong table could

### V1. `J0` (READOUTS §3, SPEC §4, amendment 1 item 5)

**The gap.** `J0` runs at one step, so it can form no class-S entry, and READOUTS gives `n_T_*` and `H_tot` class S.

**The ruling.** `J0` has no class-S entry. `n_T_A`, `n_T_B`, `H_face` and `H_tot` (which is `H_face` there) are judged by
its E twin, by the R sums and by arm 6's R energy identity. The references publish no entry of `J0` for those four, as
for `F7`'s three (amendment 1, item 3). `n_T_K`, `V_K` and the router heats stay class E, and `n_U_*` class R.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** an entry that cannot be formed is not a pass.

### V2. `F6 h=4.0 refused` (CRITERIA §4 and §8, TABLE §3)

**The gap.** A run refused at parse has no parsed definition, no ID the engine ran and no engine record, and CRITERIA
§4's "a refused run cannot pass, and so is its arm" had no exception for it.

**The ruling.** Its `inputs` and `definition` are the submitted declaration, its `solver`, `heat_id` and `operators`
those declared, its `record` the driver's own file, and its `largest_ion_change_fmol` 0.0. **The expected refusal
counts only as CRITERIA §3.7 says,** and is neither refused nor inconclusive under §4 and §8.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** arm 3 would have been inconclusive on the refusal it expects.

### V3. A retry run is registered only when owed (amendment 4, item 2)

**The gap.** Amendment 4 registered every retry run outright, so when none is owed they are missing and their arms
inconclusive.

**The ruling.**
- A retry run is registered when it is **owed:** an entry of its world (and heat) is inconclusive by order, V7's
  included, by amendment 4's naming rule. The judge's first pass writes `retry_owed.json` (layout in L2). The lead
  records its sha256 before any retry run starts, and the builder runs exactly those.
- **"Missing" is decided over all of a phase's tables together.**
- A retry run that is present and not owed is recorded, flagged and not judged.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** every arm with an S or L entry was at best inconclusive.

### V4. Arm 6 is rehearsed from the lift branch (CRITERIA §10, TABLE §4, amendment 4 item 10)

**The gap.** Arm 6's sample entry and invented runs are asked for while the router refuses a flow at a faced node, and
no final fail can rest on a bound the rehearsal has not met.

**The ruling.**
- The lift of that refusal exists as an **unlanded branch before the format sample is written.** The sample and the
  rehearsal include arm 6's kinds, run from it as labelled development runs, and the rehearsal's record names its
  revision. So arm 6's bounds meet the rehearsal before the registration tag, and §10's last rule stands with no
  exception.
- **At the phase-2 tag** the landed lift's `src` is compared with the rehearsed branch's. If a file that either one's
  arm 6 runs through differs, the arm 6 rehearsal is repeated before the phase-2 tag. No bound is corrected there: a miss
  is recorded. Phase 1 is never re-scored.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** FR-57's third line, through a bound that could be met nowhere.

### V5. Arm 7 (CRITERIA §3.8, amendment 4 item 1)

**The gap.** The map named records by the wash's own names, so it could leave out what matters, and arm 7 could pass with
little judged.

**The ruling** (layouts in L1).
- **The map is keyed by meaning.** Eight are required of every pair: `latest_sample`, `latest_supply`, `latest_heat`,
  `cumulative_sample`, `cumulative_supply`, `cumulative_heat`, `cumulative_sample_free_energy` and
  `cumulative_buffer_free_energy`; and `label_removals` where the pair's world has labels. "Per event" in §3.8 means the
  latest-event records, compared on every saved row. The builder may add meanings, compared the same way. A required
  meaning that the map does not give, or whose `mapped:<meaning>` is absent from either side's `digests`, refuses the
  pair.
- **`both_differ`** is the closed list of arrays both sides save under one name that legitimately differ, and so are
  exempt from §3.8's equal digests: identity metadata only (an ID, a fingerprint, a wall time), each with its reason, and
  never state, observation rows, ledgers or diagnostics.
- **`wash_only`: one disclosed exception.** Amendment 4's "a record of the wash is never in `wash_only`" does not hold
  for the one record that keeps a magnitude of the wash's energy bookings, a rounding scale and not physics. It may be
  listed there with its reason. No other may.
- **Each pair is bound by a hash** of the wash side's whole `definition`, equal to the strings note's (R2), and all six
  differ. The two sides' shared parts, each `definition` with the subtrees at `definition_paths` removed, hash alike.
  The canonical form is L1's. In arm 7, `definition` is the declaration the run was given, before any default is added.
- **No two runs of a phase's tables share a `record_sha256`:** runs that do are refused. The checker compares the names in
  `digests` with the arrays of the record file.
- **Arm 7 is rehearsed on one invented pair,** the builder's: a wash fixture that is none of the six. Three planted copies
  of its entries, which the judge forms, must be scored as the text says: a changed digest and a changed mapped record
  fail, and a missing router run is refused.
- **When.** The map's content is filed before the registration tag. Before the judge's tag only its layout and a
  meaningless sample are needed. This replaces "filed before the judge's tag".

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** arm 7, and so FR-57.

### V6. M1's format (CRITERIA §3.7, TABLE §3)

**The gap.** "The word `residual` and then a number" does not say what is searched, or where a unit stands.

**The ruling.** One format, found anywhere in the message:
`<ledger> ledger: residual <num> <unit>, tolerance <num> <unit>`, with one space at each place shown. `<ledger>` is a name
in the strings note, and both `<unit>`s are its unit there. `<num>` is an optional sign, digits with an optional point
and fraction, and an optional exponent. At least one occurrence is present, and every `<ledger> ledger:` with a name in
the note is followed by the rest. On P8's stops and on both expected stops one of them is the energy ledger, and on P8's
M2 reads its numbers. §3.7's |residual| > tolerance > 0 stands for every one.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** M1, M2 and P18.

### V7. An order read from differences, one of which is at rounding (CRITERIA §3.5, amendment 4 item 2)

**The gap.** The order is unresolved where either difference is at most 10 e_round, and τ alone then decides. A run
copied from another step has a zero difference, so it passed.

**The ruling.** The order is unresolved only where **both** d₁ and d₂ are at most 10 e_round. Where exactly one is, the
entry is inconclusive. A step that a retry drops is reported as inconclusive and is not claimed: the verdict states the
steps its pass covers, everything else judged on the dropped run (R, E, B, N, D) still counts, and the arm is not kept
inconclusive by it, or the retry would have no use. That a wrong coarse run is forgiven this way is among what the
comparison cannot show (D).

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes.** It is the one change to how an order reads, and an honest entry's differences lie
far above e_round by this author's estimate.

### V8. `F7`'s binding (CRITERIA §3.1 and §3.2, SPEC §1 and §8, README)

**The gap.** No link touches S, so the twins could compare two runs in which nothing binds, and pass as equal.

**The ruling.**
- **In both twins, `free_sites_S` + `bound_L_S` equals the sites,** class R, where the sites are copies ×
  sites_per_copy / 602214076 fmol (`worlds.F7.sites`; N_A × 10⁻¹⁵ exactly). Its count is 64: two half-steps of three
  stages, five roundings a stage, two amounts (60), and four reads of their stored levels. So e_round = 64 (S + 1) u ×
  the sites.
- **`bound_L_S` at the last output is above 0 in both twins.** Otherwise the twin's E entry is not formed and its run
  is inconclusive: nothing passes empty. `F7` owns both twins' entries (C8).
- **SPEC §1's Why for `F7`** reads: a node no link touches is left alone, its binding included.
- **SPEC §8's invented set** also holds a world with a site node and its no-router twin. It already has them.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** arm 3, and what a pass of it says.

### V9. Entries CRITERIA judges and READOUTS omits (READOUTS §3)

**The ruling.** CRITERIA governs. READOUTS §3 also has: arm 5's `supplied` and sums (R), arm 4's sum (R), and class N in
arms 4 and 6.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** they are judged.

## D. Statements

- **D1. How far τ can exceed the actual error.** CRITERIA §3.5's sentence holds for one branch of `est`. With errors
  C hᵠ, 3 est is 3 max(1, 2^(q−p)) (2^q − 1)/(2^p − 1) times the error at the finest step: at the cap q = 2.5, 6.6
  times at p = 2 and 39.5 at p = 1. This author's "14" at p = 1 understated it. No tolerance changes, and the judge
  reports the factor.
- **D2. Times (SPEC §0).** "Every event, switch and restart time is a multiple of the coarsest step of its world's runs,
  and a run is compared only at the outputs its step divides." The stronger claim about outputs was not true.
- **D3.** "§5" in SPEC §3 means LAWS §5.
- **D4.** README lists the amendments, `takeover_map.json` and the strings note.
- **D5. A non-finite leaf:** recorded-only, flagged; judged, a refused readout. It never refuses a table. (JSON is read
  with NaN and Infinity allowed.)
- **D6. Extra series** are allowed and recorded. Both statements in TABLE §3 read so.
- **D7.** READOUTS' "never judged" for `definition` is but for arm 7's, which C0 compares.
- **D8.** A portion-fed collector's R entries before its first portion have scale 0, so tolerance 0: the value is exactly 0.

- **Arm 5's retry** comes with arms 1 and 2's, before phase 2.
- **The judge is not re-tagged for phase 2** unless its code changes, and then by a dated erratum tagged before the phase-2

## L. Layouts

**L1. `takeover_map.json`,** version 2, and `takeover_map_rehearsal.json` for the rehearsal's pair:

```json
{"kind": "liquid-routing takeover map, version 2",
 "pairs": {"<pair>": {
   "definition_paths": ["<JSON pointer to a subtree>"],
   "records": {"<meaning>": {"wash": "<array>", "router": "<array>", "index": null}},
   "both_differ": {"<array>": "<reason>"},
   "wash_only": {"<array>": "<reason>"}, "router_only": {"<array>": "<reason>"}}}}
```

- `index` has one item per axis of the router's array (an integer selects there, null keeps), or is null for the whole. Each
  side's `digests` holds
  `mapped:<meaning>`, the selected part in the wash record's shape and type, and the two are equal. Every other array that
  one side alone saves is in `wash_only` or `router_only` with its reason.
- A pointer that resolves on a side is removed there; one that resolves on neither side is a fault.
- **Canonical form.** JSON of the object with keys sorted, separators `,` and `:`, UTF-8 with no escaping of non-ASCII, no
  NaN; every number that is not a boolean written as `repr(float(x))`; the sha256 of its bytes. Pointers are resolved against the whole object before
  any removal, and removed deepest and highest index first.

**L2. `retry_owed.json`** (the judge's): `{"kind": "liquid-routing retry owed, version 1", "phase": 1, "runs": ["<name>"]}`,
`phase` 1 or 2, the names sorted.

**L3. The strings note:** `registered_strings.json`, `registered_strings_phase2.json` and
`registered_strings_rehearsal.json`, in `validation/liquid_routing/`:

```json
{"kind": "liquid-routing registered strings, version 1",
 "revision": "<40 hex>", "lift_revision": null,
 "ids": {"solver": "", "heat_exact": "", "heat_default": "", "router": "", "wash": "", "label_owner": "",
         "site_operator": ""},
 "operators": {"<run name>": ["<sorted IDs>"]},
 "ledgers": {"<ledger>": "<unit>"},
 "pairs": {"<pair>": "<64 hex>"}}
```

- All values are strings (`lift_revision` may be null, and is the lift branch's 40 hex in the rehearsal's). IDs are in the
  new form, as the rename's committed mapping gives them at the tag. `operators` has every registered run of its phase,
  exactly and sorted; a retry run's is its world's. In the rehearsal's file it has every invented run and `pairs` holds the
  builder's pair. `pairs` is empty in the phase-2 file.
- The `engine_revision` check reads `revision`, and for arm 6's rehearsal runs `lift_revision`. This replaces amendment 4
  item 8's exception: the rehearsal's revision is checked against its own file.
- The judge parses a meaningless sample of each layout (L1, L3) before its tag.

**L4. The reference files, as published:**
- **Every file holds every top-level key,** empty where it has none.
- **`cases`** also hold `accuracy`, an object with the same keys. **Arm 1's file** has `worlds`, holding t = 0 alone (the
  two levels, class R, and `H_face`, class E); a value at h belongs to a `case`. Its `events` is empty.
- **Arm 2's event blocks** hold `class`, `time_s` and `accuracy` beside `before`, `sample`, `supplied` and `heat`. **Arm 4's
  entries** hold `amount` (one number as a string, not by species), `volume_pL`, `loss`, `resident`, `arriving`, `time_s`
  and `accuracy` (all five), and `class` for `amount`, `loss` and `volume_pL`. The table's `amount` is by species and its
  `volume` is the same quantity as `volume_pL`.
- **`planted_series`:** P5 `H1` with `H_A`; P7 `<world>|<step>` with `H_B` and `H_tot`; P9 `G1` with `loss`; P11 `F1L|8.0`
  with the three `label_T_tag_*`; P14 and P15 `<world>|<step>` with `n_T_1` and `n_T_2`; P19 `J1|<step>` with `n_T_A`.
- **`plants`** rows hold `plant`, `check`, `class`, `world`, `time_s`, `step_s`, `honest`, `planted`, `distance`,
  `expected_tolerance`, `margin` and `no_margin`. Class-S rows hold more.
- **Invented names:** an invented world is a registered name after `inv-` (nineteen of them, `inv-F7` and its no-router
  run among them). A run keeps the registered form with its own step in the name (`inv-F1 h=8.25`,
  `inv-N-buffered exact j=0`). Invented plant rows name `world` and `step_s`.
- **`accuracy`** is absolute, to three digits, rounded up.
