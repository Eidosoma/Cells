# Amendment 4, 25 November 2026: what could pass with nothing judged, or be refused for no fault, and the spec auditor

**Written by the registration's author at the lead's request, after amendment 3** (d5d4c8ed), before the judge
starts and before any registered run. Items 1 to 8 are gaps of this author's text, found by a second reading of
TABLE and CRITERIA run type by run type, and adopted by the lead. Item 10 is the lead's.

- **Bands.** It changes no constant, count, window, class, scale or plant. Items 1 to 5 and 7 change what is
  registered, refused, unscorable or inconclusive.
- **Value-silent.** It states no reference, engine or planted value.
- **Precedence.** It governs over the tagged files and amendments 1 to 3 where they differ. Items 6 and 10 are lines
  of the protocol's §3 and §5.

## 1. Arm 7's runs, and nothing passes empty (CRITERIA §3.8, §4, §6.3 and §8; TABLE §3)

**The gap.** `inputs.json` lists no arm 7 run, §4 ignores a name it does not list, and §8 passes an arm in which
nothing failed. So arm 7 could pass with no run.

**The rulings.**
- **Nothing passes empty.** An arm, or a request's line, with no judged entry formed is inconclusive, never a pass. A
  plant whose named entry is not formed is unscorable, never caught.
- **Arm 7's registered runs** are `<pair> wash` and `<pair> router` for the six pairs of `inputs.json`'s
  `placement`, 12 runs of role `judged`. A missing one is refused.
- **Their entries.** `world` is the pair, `inputs` is `{}`, and `series` may be empty. `step_s`, `steps`, `t`,
  `solver`, `heat` and `heat_id` are equal on the two sides of a pair.
- **C0 compares the two `definition`s** of a pair leaf by leaf. They differ only at paths that
  `takeover_map.json` names, and a named path lies only in the wash's or the router's own declaration and its
  controls (the checker confirms that). Any other difference refuses the pair, as does a `definition` that is missing.
- **A pair in which no array is matched, or no wash record is mapped, is refused.**

**`takeover_map.json`** (`validation/liquid_routing/takeover_map.json`, the builder's), by name:

```json
{"kind": "liquid-routing takeover map, version 1",
 "pairs": {"<pair>": {"definition_paths": ["<JSON pointer to a subtree>"],
   "records": {"<wash record>": {"router": "<router record>", "index": null}},
   "wash_only": {"<array>": "<reason>"}, "router_only": {"<array>": "<reason>"}}}}
```

- **A mapped record** may have more axes on the router's side. `index` selects the part, a list of integers, or is
  null for the whole. The router side's `digests` holds that part under `mapped:<wash record>`, in the wash record's
  shape and type, and it equals the wash side's `<wash record>`.
- **An array only one side saves** is mapped, or listed in `wash_only` or `router_only` with its reason. A record of
  the wash is never in `wash_only`.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** arm 7, and so FR-57. It removes a pass that rested on nothing.

## 2. The retry's runs (CRITERIA §2, §4 and §7; TABLE §1 and §2)

**The gap.** No retry name is in `inputs.json`, so every retry run would have been ignored.

**The ruling.** They are registered by rule, with role `judged`, and echo their world's block at their own step:

| Arm | Run | Step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `<start> exact j=4 retry`, every start | 1/8 s |
| 1 | `<start> default j=4 retry`, for `eq-mid`, `eq-hi`, `r4-mid` and `r4-hi` | 1/128 s |
| 2 | `<world> <heat> j=3 retry`, for the three worlds; `N-none`'s also with either of the other two | 1/8 s |
| 5 | `<world> h=0.03125 retry`, for `H1` to `H4` | 1/32 s |
| 6 | `J1 h=0.0625 retry` | 1/16 s |

- **One retry run serves every inconclusive entry of its world** (and heat, in arms 1 and 2). Entries that passed or
  failed keep their score. The retry run's own R, E, B, N and D entries are judged, and a fail there counts.
- **Its tables:** `table-retry/` for arms 1, 2 and 5, and `table-arm6-retry/` for arm 6.
- **CRITERIA §4's row** "a run name `inputs.json` does not list" means one that neither it nor items 1 and 2 register.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** without it, an inconclusive entry could never be resolved.

## 3. The no-router support runs (CRITERIA §4, TABLE §3)

**The gap.** `F7 h=1.0 no router` and `J0 h=0.25 no router` echo a world block that declares links, an inlet and a
collector, and TABLE asks for router readouts they cannot have.

**The ruling.** Their echo has `links_pL_per_s` `[]`, `inlets_mM` `{}` and `collectors` `[]`, and is the world's block
otherwise. Their required series are only those of their twin row (CRITERIA §3.1). Any other is recorded.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** arms 3 and 6 would have been inconclusive on an honest run.

## 4. The restart, and every twin (CRITERIA §3.1, TABLE §3)

**The gap.** TABLE says `t` starts at 0, which a resumed run's need not. The checkpoint's time was not checked. "The
outputs they share" could be none.

**The ruling.**
- `G5 h=1.0 restart`: `t` may start at 4.0 s, and `restart.time_s` must be 4.0, or the run is refused. Every registered
  output after 4.0 s is present and compared. Its row at 4.0 s is compared if it holds one. `restart` is null in every
  other run.
- **Every twin** is compared at every registered output of its row (the rows to 16 s inclusive for `N-none`; after
  4.0 s for the restart). A registered output missing on either side is a refused readout.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** an equality could have been passed by comparing nothing.

**Can a verdict depend on it? Yes:** a departing one refuses its run, and no honest run could have been matched before.

## 10. The spec auditor (the protocol's §3 and §5)

- **Who.** An agent who did not write this text. It reads only this folder and builds nothing else: it is not the
  references agent, the builder, the judge or the checker.
- **What it does.** It writes a reader skeleton, and a fabricated honest table for every registered run. It reports
  what is refused, what passes with nothing judged, and what an honest run would fail.
- **When.** It starts from this amendment's tag. It reports before the references' tag if its findings change anything
  they publish, and always before the judge's tag.
- **Its questions** reach this author only through the lead, and a finding changes the text only by a dated amendment.
- **Its tables and numbers** are its own. No references agent, builder, judge or checker reads them, and no
  tolerance, reference or verdict depends on one.

**Can a verdict depend on it? No,** except by the amendments its findings lead to, before the registration tag.
