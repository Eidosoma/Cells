# Vesicle rupture rates and retained cargo during shrinkage

## First rupture under a tension hold

FR-31 asks for rates at several tensions. Karal and Yamazaki measured time to complete aspiration of pure DOPC vesicles in matched 0.1 M sugar solutions. At each tension and temperature, the authors fitted survival against time in three independent experiments, each with 20 vesicles. Figure 2a gives the mean fitted rate and its standard error. Two temperatures have measurements at all three tensions. [Figure 2a, journal p. 081103-3](https://shizuoka.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/8029/files/150908002.pdf#page=4) · [Figure readings and error bars](../../checks/data/karal2015_rupture_rates.json)

| Temperature | Rate at 7.0 mN/m | Rate at 7.5 mN/m | Rate at 8.0 mN/m | Middle rate from the two endpoints |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ≈25 °C | 0.00263 s⁻¹ | 0.00556 s⁻¹ | 0.0153 s⁻¹ | 0.00634 s⁻¹ |
| ≈32 °C | 0.00423 s⁻¹ | 0.00813 s⁻¹ | 0.0230 s⁻¹ | 0.00986 s⁻¹ |

Rates in the three measurement columns are exponentiated marker readings. The last column assumes a straight line in log rate against tension and takes the geometric mean of the endpoint rates. It exceeds the observed middle rate by about 14% at 25 °C and 21% at 32 °C. This retrospective check gives FR-31 a measured middle point at each temperature against which to compare a proposed law. Figure 1a's illustrated survival curves and Figure 2a's replicate means come from the same study. The deterministic vesicle representation (OL6) is a separate project dependency. ## Independent fixed-tension series

Evans and Smith (2011) provide a candidate from a different laboratory. Their appendix describes steady suction ramps and suction steps, determines tension from suction and the pipette and vesicle radii, and follows individual vesicles from first stress to optical disappearance. The preparations included DOPC and SOPC. Their clock includes the approach to a tension plateau. The public methods inspected here leave open which lipid cohorts had sustained plateaus, when each plateau began, the individual vesicle areas and how surviving vesicles were censored. The screening found no event-level deposit pairing those fields. [Evans and Smith, Appendix AI.1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3182099/#S13)

Tazawa and Yamazaki (2023) explicitly studied constant tension. The institutional record lists the article, and the supplement combines methods with a rupture-rate analysis. Independence of the DOPC controls from earlier work in the same laboratory and the availability of event-level records remain unresolved. Levadny et al. (2013) is a further bibliographic lead whose methods could not be inspected in this search. [Tazawa and Yamazaki, institutional record](https://shizuoka.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/14487) · [Levadny et al., primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/la304662p)

The Cells biological-evidence thread screened three further sources. The Huang et al. Dryad record lists vesicle aspiration and size materials, but the study increased suction until rupture. Santinho et al. measured radius and used short steps for elastic equilibration before ramping suction to rupture. Zhelev and Needham applied pipette tension and initiated pores electrically, so the clock follows an imposed initiation. None of the three records establishes a held-tension risk set with individual rupture or censoring records and linked vesicle sizes. [Huang et al., data record](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.cjsxksnmm) · [Huang et al., experiment](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2516407123) · [Santinho et al., methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48086-7) · [Zhelev and Needham](https://doi.org/10.1016/0005-2736(93)90319-U)

These entries identify candidates for a separate fixed-tension series. The search used bibliographic and archive metadata and bounded methods text. The mixed supplement stayed unopened. D287 reserves extraction of the designated Karal temperature columns for a sealed reader after the model and tolerance are fixed. ## A pore can reseal in the same vesicle

Oglęcka et al. imaged **three consecutive pore openings in one vesicle** containing POPC, sphingomyelin and cholesterol under strong hypotonic swelling. Each pore resealed within one second. In a separate study, Chabanon et al. imaged a fluorescent glucose analogue leaving a vesicle through an open pore as its radius dropped, followed by resealing. The amount of sucrose lost remains unmeasured. [Oglęcka et al., Figure 5](https://elifesciences.org/articles/03695/figures#fig5) · [Chabanon et al., Figure 4B–D and Movie S7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5406380/#fig4)

The assembly model ends the lineage at its first terminal lysis and returns the contents to the pool. Changing the timing of that terminal event alone still gives at most one event per lineage. Repeated partial leakage and survival of the same vesicle would require a distinct outcome if Cells takes on this regime. Oglęcka's external bath was deionized water. The model requires positive external osmolarity. The comparison concerns the observed sequence of events. [Assembly lysis and material return](https://github.com/Eidosoma/Cells/blob/f1783c558a79379d9aab846a48165b4833f2282f/src/cells/models/assembly_lineages_v2/population.py#L613) · [Osmotic law and bath domain](https://github.com/Eidosoma/Cells/blob/f1783c558a79379d9aab846a48165b4833f2282f/src/cells/models/assembly_lineages_v2/lumen.py#L70)

## Intact vesicles in a more concentrated bath

Fujiwara and Yanagisawa measured both shrinkage and internal green fluorescent protein (GFP). After transfer from an internal osmolarity of 70 to a bath of 285 mOsm/L, five tracked liposomes reached an initial-to-final volume ratio of about **4** around 320 s. Figure S2 shows individual 10-minute ratios spanning approximately 3.3–4.9. Figure S3 pairs radius-derived volume with tracer concentration within the same liposome. At the last circle, both inverse volume and GFP concentration were about **3.3 times** their initial normalized values. Multiplying concentration by volume gives approximately the initial amount of GFP. The authors report a linear-fit R² of 0.9987. Pointwise retention errors are unavailable. [Fujiwara and Yanagisawa, Figures 2–3](https://doi.org/10.1021/sb4001917) · [Figures S2–S3](https://doi.org/10.1021/sb4001917.s001)

With fixed internal osmoles and an initially balanced lumen, the compiled Cells fixture gives **4.0714** for the same volume ratio, or **0.2456** for final over initial volume. The ratio follows directly from 285/70 without fitting. The two static engine endpoints and the observed plateau are compatible at the precision reported. The experiment also measured the time to shrink. The assembly law computes an equilibrium volume and supplies no relaxation time. Volume reconstruction uses apparent diameter, and retention of GFP alone leaves the smaller osmoles unaccounted for. [Replay inputs and result](../../checks/data/fujiwara2014_vesicle.json) · [Compiled fixture](../../checks/vesicle.py#L117)

This retention evidence belongs to the Fujiwara specimens. In Bhatia et al., external calcein marked bath liquid in an inward tube. Their final outer-volume uncertainty of about 1,000 µm³ spans the approximately 950 µm³ difference between a cylindrical-tube reconstruction and the retained-solute prediction. The Bhatia comparison remains inconclusive. The four lower-than-predicted reduced volumes of Wolfisberg et al. remain a separate measured disagreement.
