# Lean verification options for Cells engine laws

At Cells revision `3f84e002`, the best first experiment is the gap junction in `resolved_pump_tissue@3`. Its direction convention has a physical interpretation, its accounting generalizes to a tissue of any size, and an existing test already reverses the endpoints in the JAX kernel. For a chemistry-first experiment, the closed enzyme reaction fixture offers an equally concrete starting point. ## What existing Lean projects establish

- **Biological definitions.** Jones and Josephson proved properties of transcription and translation in Lean 4. Trying to prove that complementary coding and template strands yield the same RNA exposed an error in their definitions. Their published decoder also maps an unrecognized input to STOP. For Cells, the lesson is to state what counts as an admissible input and to check a relationship between two independently meaningful quantities. [Genetics preprint, especially §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36343v1) · [Decoder source](https://github.com/ATOMSLab/LeanGenetics/blob/ddb1752e32e8ce2230fd453e56ec3460fcad361e/GeneticCode/GeneticCode.lean)
- **Reaction accounting.** VeriBiota gives a worked Lean proof that a reaction firing preserves a weighted total when the products and reactants have equal weight. Its example preserves total population during infection and recovery. The Lean 4 chemical reaction network library `crnt-lean` represents each reaction by a stoichiometric vector and proves that the rate of change lies in their span. The first pattern fits discrete events. The second fits Cells’ continuous reaction extents. [VeriBiota invariants](https://omnisgenomics.github.io/VeriBiota/invariants/) · [CRNT foundations](https://github.com/marpaia/crnt-lean/blob/master/docs/foundations.md) · [CRNT kinetics](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marpaia/crnt-lean/master/docs/dynamics.md)
- **Chemical laws and numerical evidence.** Bobbin and colleagues formalized adsorption and gas-law derivations, making assumptions such as nonzero divisors explicit. Their published code uses Lean 3. A later Lean 4 project, LeanBET, uses one algorithm over real numbers for proofs and floating point for computations. Its authors report machine-precision agreement with the Python reference on 18 of 19 adsorption datasets and a 0.03% difference on the nineteenth. That is a precedent for reporting a theorem alongside a measured discrepancy. [Chemical physics paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.12150v5) · [Authors’ code and version](https://atomslab.github.io/LeanChemicalTheories/) · [LeanBET paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16169)

## Where a proof fits in Cells

Cells already has the registry proposed in the inspiration note. The `cells.declarations@2` manifest identifies registered mechanisms, their host models, declarations and available units, signs and test references. The tissue model binds its declared structure to array kernels. In its explicit transport calculation it derives concentrations from amounts and volumes, combines junction availability with the gate, and calls the surface transport kernel. That kernel evaluates the flux and assigns it to the owning cells. The law ID, call site and existing fixture together identify what a theorem would cover. ## First experiment with a gap junction

1. **Prove the direction.** Let `J(a,b,v)` be the signed amount of one ion crossing per second from cell A to cell B, with concentrations `a,b` and voltage difference `v = φA − φB`. On the admitted domain, exchanging endpoints with the same route parameters should give `J(b,a,−v) = −J(a,b,v)`. At equal voltage, positive area, permeability and opening, `a > b` must give a strictly positive flux. This second statement catches a backwards law or a law that always returns zero. Both would pass a global conservation check. [Flux kernel](../src/cells/laws/junctions/selective.py#L19) · [Zero-voltage branch](../src/cells/laws/electrochemistry.py#L17)
2. **Prove the accounting.** For any finite graph with valid endpoints, each internal junction books the same signed flux once with a minus at its sender and once with a plus at its receiver. Summing the junction contributions over cells gives zero for each species, even when several junctions share a cell. Weighting by valence and Faraday’s constant gives the corresponding charge balance. These are balances of *amount*. Cells of different volumes can have different concentration changes. Extending the claim to a tissue exchanging material with a held bath requires the signed reservoir supply. [Owner updates and reservoir accounting](../src/cells/numerics/surface_transport.py#L310)
3. **Connect the theorem to an engine run.** Use a pinned Lean 4 and Mathlib pair to check the exact statements without proof placeholders. Exercise the existing gradient, reversed, blocked and padded fixtures against the independent numerical reference, then use the tissue model’s existing per-cell ledger check. A deliberately swapped sender/receiver booking in comparison data should fail the local check even though its global sum is zero. Record the proof result and the numerical residual with its existing tolerance against the same checkout revision and normal snapshot. The theorem covers the stated real-arithmetic law. Those comparisons supply evidence about the compiled engine. ## Other options

| Candidate | First claim worth proving | Scope and cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closed reactions in `local_species_reactions@1` on `resolved_pump_composite@1` | For moiety counts `M`, stoichiometry `N` and reaction extents `ξ`, `MN = 0` implies `M(n + Nξ) = Mn`. In the existing `E + S ⇌ C`, `C → E + P` fixture, enzyme amount `E + C` and substrate amount `S + C + P` are conserved. | Small algebraic proof with wide reuse. Read integer coefficients from the declared fixture and check each column in Lean. Sources and sinks contribute their own terms. The operator already checks integer balance and computes each node’s change from its extents. [Admission](../src/cells/operators/species_reactions.py#L431) · [Half-step](../src/cells/operators/species_reactions.py#L1024) · [Fixture](../validation/tissue/species_reactions_fixtures.py#L248) |
| Reversible mass action in the same operator | For positive activities and a nonnegative rate scale, the net rate times the dimensionless affinity is nonnegative, and the rate vanishes at zero affinity. | A small thermodynamic proof that can catch a reversed driving force. The numerical comparison should include states on both sides of equilibrium, where the implementation uses `expm1`. [Rate and affinity](../src/cells/laws/reactions/mass_action.py#L6) · [Passive relaxation test](../tests/unit/test_species_reactions_halfstep.py#L146) |
| Error bounds for `constant_field@1` in `resolved_pump_tissue@3` | Bound the error of the Bernoulli polynomial used for `abs(x) < 10⁻⁴`, including the value at zero. | More work. A truncation bound covers the analytic approximation. An end-to-end bound would also need rounding, the executable’s operation order and a stated input range. [Piecewise implementation](../src/cells/laws/electrochemistry.py#L17) · [JAX numerical behavior](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/faq.html#jit-changes-the-exact-numerics-of-outputs) |

The junction heat regression shows why theorem scope matters. The default ARS(2,2,2) solver has a negative quadrature weight. In a two-cell fixture where the voltage crosses zero, it can book a negative heat increment and the engine refuses the step. The trapezoid option passes that case. A claim about nonnegative instantaneous dissipation and a claim about heat booked over a step therefore need different hypotheses. Model calibration still needs independent biological measurements.
