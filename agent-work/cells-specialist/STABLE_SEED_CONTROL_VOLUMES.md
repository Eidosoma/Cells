# Prospective fixed-seed control volumes

This new prefix constructs a bounded Voronoi partition from retained binary64
seed coordinates and owner IDs. It preserves the complete original failed
source request and explicitly replaces dual vertices, cell rings and shared
edge connectivity. It does not reposition seeds, overwrite old arrays or
inherit metric, operator, physical-cell, environment or loop qualification.

The native source first creates Delaunay faces, optionally merges them, and
collects incident face centroids when `use_centroids` is true. Boundary points
are point reflections of a face centre through a primal-edge midpoint. The
boundary-edge lookup uses the earlier directed-only mapping. The resulting
cell vertices are angularly sorted and deduplicated; selected dual edges later
come from nearest-midpoint pairing. These statements describe the pinned
source, not a claim that one mechanism explains all five observed failures.
Neither that centroid construction nor its corrected integer topology enforces
the nearest-seed half-plane inequalities. Topology02 deliberately retains the
original coordinates and cell rings, so it cannot repair a layout defect.

The five retained failed layouts contain 37 owners, including 20 outside
owners. The earlier six-layout panel contains 50 owners because it also includes
the 13-owner passing square-zero layout. Those original failures remain immutable.
A future successful reconstruction would be a separately declared geometry law,
not a reclassification of those failed layouts or a native-equivalence claim.

The proposed domain is an explicitly declared finite, strictly convex CCW
polygon containing every retained seed strictly. Its provenance must be
recorded. Native mesh `xyaxis` uses a 1.1 plotting expansion and is not a tissue
boundary. The first three failed inputs contain explicit environment
`bounds_m`; adopting them as a prospective control-volume envelope changes the
geometry law and does not establish tissue equivalence. The two lattice-seeded
ring/centre caller inputs do not directly supply that envelope in the saved
record. Their nominal
`world_len_m` is insufficient: the reached native lattice bounds use point
extrema expanded by the radius. No default margin or `[0,L]` substitute is
introduced.

Production will intersect the explicit domain with exact rational nearest-seed
half-planes by successive polygon clipping. The independent reference will
enumerate pairs of constraint lines, retain feasible intersections and order
the resulting convex hull, without importing the production clipping or
admission kernels. Degenerate multiple-constraint vertices retain all owners;
duplicate seed positions refuse. Collinear and cocircular seeds require no
triangulation, perturbation, epsilon or arbitrary diagonal.

Global exact vertices are deduplicated by rational coordinate equality and
rounded once per shared vertex with the pinned stable scalar primitive. All
cell rings use that shared coordinate store. Exact and rounded complexes must
independently satisfy containment, reciprocal shared edges, CCW positive areas,
no overlap, complete domain-boundary coverage and total-area conservation.
Rounding collisions, lost containment, nonconformity or a rounded boundary
leaving the declared domain refuse with full partial evidence. No tolerance is
widened to accept a changed domain. Exact Voronoi targets remain distinct from
the actual shared binary64 mesh and its geometric moments.

The pinned conservative polygon helpers supply production admission and exact
moments only; no height, extrusion, bath, physical membrane or initializer is
fabricated. Integrated edge normals and incidence must cancel on internal
edges, close for constant fields, and satisfy affine-field divergence/area
identities. Rounded scalar storage defects are retained with bounds from
independent exact targets. Physical admission remains closed even after those
prospective checks pass.
