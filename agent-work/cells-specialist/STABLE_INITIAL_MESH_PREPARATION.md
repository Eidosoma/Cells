equivalence. Fresh source replay separately reconstructs original caller kwargs.

The geometric lane recomputes every repaired primitive independently and checks
the complete supplied primal/dual geometry and cache incidence. Integer-grid
predicates expose crossings, overlap, nonmanifold incidence and boundary defects;

paired dual-edge geometry. The retained-complex boundary is distinguished from

exact empty-circle/tie diagnostics without claiming independent topology creation.

specific remaining risks: a defect in either leaves this candidate open.

eleven fresh independent geometry records and one bounded finalization unit.
