## What the full RHS adds to the previous contact map

The source's 17-row network packet has six evolving coordinates per cell:
Na/K/Cl and the three regulatory coordinates. P/Snail/ST are fixed context in
this profile; free-miRNA and bound-complex rows are reconstructed plotting caches.
The native RHS refreshes deliberately stale finite caches, leaves all primary
and fixed rows unchanged, returns zero cache-row slopes, and restores the
temporarily borrowed miRNA drift parameters. The independent expression is pure.

Active/blocked, ionic-only and miRNA-only controls expose the actual coupling
routes. Gate parameters are algebraic and oriented; asymmetric endpoint reversal
alone changes the model, while swapping the hemichannels restores the same
primary slopes and reverses the oriented transfer flux. Prescribed leak blocks
do not implicitly block pumps, whereas zero base membrane permeability does.

The source uses miRNA transport valence -1 but excludes all regulatory charge
from voltage. Its free proxy equals the total-miRNA primary coordinate, so the
binding construction is not exact physical miRNA inventory accounting. At the
two prepared cells the extra bound-miRNA contribution would be approximately
24.19086 and 74.37355 source coordinates; it must not be silently added to the
voltage closure or interpreted as a verified molecular mass balance.

The first profile intentionally excludes endogenous cancer K-expression,
neighbor effects and contact expression isolation/overexpression. It is therefore
not a general reproduction of the cancer pilot or the continuing memory/relay
assays. Their specific switches, preparation and full-state boundary operations
remain separate gates.
